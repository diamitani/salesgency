⚡ 3 Ingestion Trigger Options

1. Skill to Webhook: Upload a SalesGency skill to your LLM; send prospect data directly to the automation via webhook.
2. Daily Trigger: Scheduled pull from your CRM or spreadsheet at a set time each morning.
3. Custom Agent: Fully autonomous — scans for ICP-matched companies, builds prospect lists, and runs the full process without human input.

🎯 2 Automation Types

1. Company Automation: You have a target company list but no contacts — the system finds decision makers based on personas, enriches data, and reaches out.
2. Contact Automation: You already have contacts in a spreadsheet or CRM — the system skips the people-find step and goes straight to research and personalized email.
Both types support optional enrichment if you only have names, LinkedIn URLs, or partial data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need n8n?

For the Custom Engagement and DIY Package, yes — n8n is the automation backbone. SalesGency can help you set up your self-hosted n8n instance as part of the engagement. For the Custom Agent, SalesGency can host the environment for you.

What if I don't have any contact data?

The Company Automation path is designed for exactly this scenario. You provide a list of target companies (or an ICP description), and the system finds, validates, and enriches contact information before reaching out.

Can I use my existing CRM?

Yes. All major CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Pipedrive, Zoho, Monday, ClickUp) are supported. The CRM Dedupe Shield will always check your existing records before any contact is touched.

What data tools do I need?

The minimum viable stack is one enrichment tool (Clay, Apollo, or ZoomInfo) and one outreach platform. SalesGency can advise on the best combination based on your budget and ICP.
