How an engagement works

A 90-day activation. Then we make it yours.

Six months, fixed monthly engagement. The first 90 days prove the system on a real pilot; months four through six make it durable and transfer it to your team.

01

Activation workshop — 60–90 minutes

We confirm the business outcome and decision-maker, walk your current process end to end, identify systems and owners, score the six activations by value and readiness, and select the first pilot with its baseline and acceptance evidence.

02

90-day activation — pilot to production

We build the first workflows against your real CRM, run a controlled pilot, review errors and responses with you, and promote to production only when the acceptance evidence passes. Health reporting starts with the first workflow — never bolted on later.

03

Optimize and transfer — months 4–6

We expand to the remaining activations, harden controls, and hand over operator playbooks, training, and the run ledger. Your team owns the system; we stay available, not required.
