Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with SalesGency as your in-house GTM AI automation agency.

It means we don't hold your workflows hostage inside a proprietary black box. Everything we build—the n8n workflows, prompt skills, custom agents, CRM formatters, and playbooks—is deployed inside your accounts and company infrastructure. You own the intellectual property and can operate it indefinitely.
Every project is engineered around three layers: Workflows provide deterministic routing and CRM sync rails; Skills store specialized domain knowledge like PAS copywriting and lead scoring formulas; and Agents provide autonomous reasoning to classify incoming requests and route opportunities in real time.
We integrate natively with Salesforce, HubSpot, Close, Pipedrive, and custom PostgreSQL databases. On the automation side, we leverage n8n, Make, Clay, Smartlead, Instantly, Apollo, Retell, OpenAI, Anthropic, and custom API webhooks.
When a qualified lead replies or submits a form, our Inbound Automation workflow routes the enriched lead data simultaneously to both the assigned account executive and a leadership oversight inbox. This ensures sub-minute reply times and prevents valuable prospects from slipping through the cracks.
Our standard sprint is 30 days from kickoff to live production handover. During this window, we audit, architect, test, and train your team with video playbooks and reference key sheets.
