The 8-Stage Prospect Automation Engine

Our canonical 9-node n8n autonomous graph. Eight deterministic stages, one unified pipeline running continuously inside your sales stack.

01 Signal

Target

Intent-based company pull triggered by buying signals (funding rounds, job openings, tech stack adoption), never bought lists.

02 Safety

Shield

CRM Dedupe Shield halts on active deals, recent touches, competitors, and exclusions. Zero rep collisions by construction.

03 Routing

Assign

Territory round-robin routes qualified accounts to the correct rep or pod automatically so no account is worked twice.

04 Waterfall

Reveal

Multi-provider contact waterfall (Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo) with live SMTP deliverability verification before sequencing.

05 Scoring

Rank

Contacts are scored by seniority, persona match, and buying authority. Only the top decision-maker moves to copywriting.

06 Intelligence

Research

Autonomous AI agent synthesizes company news, tech debt, and pain points, writing a verified hypothesis back to the CRM.

07 AI PAS

Write

Generates hyper-personalized Problem-Agitate-Solve emails under 85 words, zero buzzwords, queued for human review.

08 Telemetry

Sync & Audit

CRM write-back, staggered sequencer enrollment, and complete immutable run ledger recording every step for governance.
