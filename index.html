How it works

The engine's eight stages, every run.

This is the actual production architecture — taken from the deployed workflow blueprints, not a diagram drawn for marketing. A scheduled run executes all eight stages; the inbound engine runs the mirror image on every form submission.

01

Target — intent-based company pull

The run starts inside your CRM, pulling companies showing buying intent — funding, hiring, expansion signals — not a purchased list.

02

Shield — collision & ICP filters

Companies with open deals, recent sales activity, competitors, and out-of-ICP segments are removed before a single contact is touched. Zero rep collisions, by construction.

03

Assign — territory round-robin

Surviving accounts are assigned across your territories automatically, so coverage is even and no account is worked twice.

04

Reveal — contact waterfall

Multi-provider email and phone waterfall with live verification. Bad addresses never enter a sequence — that's how sender reputation stays intact.

05

Rank — best contacts first

Contacts are scored and ranked per account; only the top decision-makers move forward. One decision-maker per company, capped.

06

Research — AI pain-point hypothesis

An AI agent researches each account and drafts a pain-point hypothesis, written back to the CRM where your reps can see and correct it.

07

Write — personalized PAS emails

AI agents draft problem-agitate-solve emails and subject lines per contact — researched, under 85 words, zero buzzwords — queued for human approval.

08

Sync — CRM write-back & handoff

Approved contacts, notes, and hypotheses sync to the CRM; sequences enroll with staggered inbox load-balancing. Every step logged to the run ledger.

The inbound engine runs the mirror image on every form submission: an AI agent classifies intent into seven categories (speak with sales, partnerships, careers, press, support…), sets the MQL lifecycle stage, routes partners, drafts a grounded reply to sales-ready leads — and attributes every downstream deal to the exact workflow execution that created it.
