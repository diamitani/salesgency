The Sales Ops
Setup Guide.

We deploy autonomous revenue architecture in 30 days. Here is our exact blueprint from audit to production.

Pipeline
Audit → Design → Deploy.
We follow a strict, four-phase setup guide to ensure your engines go live without disrupting current sales momentum.
PHASE 01

Revenue Stack Audit & JTBD

We analyze your current CRM, tech stack, and lead flow. We interview your top reps using Jobs-To-Be-Done to find exactly where manual data entry and lead routing are causing bottlenecks.

PHASE 02

Blueprint & Tech Design

We design the custom logic. We determine whether to use n8n, Make.com, or custom Python agents. You receive a complete visual diagram of all data flows before we write a single line of code.

PHASE 03

Integration & Testing

We build the engines and connect them securely to your Salesforce, HubSpot, or Outreach instances. We run shadowed tests to ensure the AI logic routes and enriches perfectly.

PHASE 04

Handoff & Playbooks

We deploy to production. Your team receives the "Sales Ops Playbook" detailing how the engines work, how to monitor them, and how to scale the prompts as your ICP evolves.
