Skills, Agents, Workflows:
The Delivery Blueprint

Each of our projects are designed to give you three.
We expand on what you have to deploy an autonomous revenue engine that lives in your infrastructure and compounds over time.

Three Interlocking Layers

Why Every Project Delivers The Complete Triad

Most automation projects fail because they build isolated pieces. We engineer the complete triad so your revenue operations run smoothly without maintenance headaches.

Layer 01 · Rail

Workflows

Deterministic Highway

The plumbing connecting your sales channels, databases, CRMs, and email rotators. Triggered by webhooks, cron intervals, or rep actions with error handling and live audit logging.

Key Architecture:
Webhook Ingest Dual Inbox Dispatch CRM Stage Sync Deduplication Failure Alerting
Layer 02 · Knowledge

Skills

Domain Intelligence Recipes

Packaged operational blueprints that instruct AI how to evaluate context. Contains your specific ICP rules, PAS copywriting formulas, data transformation routines, and buyer intent filters.

Key Architecture:
PAS Copy Engine Signal Extractors MQL Classifiers Quote Compiler Cleanse Rules
Layer 03 · Autonomy

Agents

Autonomous Decision Engines

Autonomous runtime workers that parse incoming conversations, classify sentiment, answer technical questions, schedule meetings, and escalate hot leads to your senior account executives.

Key Architecture:
Inbound Chatbot Reply Triage Agent Signal Researcher Calendar Booker Support Router
⏱️ The Delali Development Cycle

Our 30-Day Deployment Sprint

From initial discovery audit to live production handover, every step is rigorously tested and documented.

01

Week 1: Audit & Signal Mapping

We analyze your sales tech stack, CRM schemas, inbound conversion points, and buyer trigger signals to design the technical architecture document.

02

Week 2: Triad Architecture Build

We construct the deterministic workflows (n8n/Make), author the dynamic prompt skills, and train the custom agents tailored to your sales playbook.

03

Week 3: CRM & Dual Inbox Routing

We integrate bi-directional CRM syncing, automated import formatters, quote generators, and dual inbox notifications with end-to-end event telemetry.

04

Week 4: Playbooks, Training & Handover

We organize your master key sheets, deliver custom video courses and rep SOPs, conduct live shadow sessions, and transfer complete infrastructure ownership.

Engineering Invariant

The PAL Protocol: Parse, Scan, Intent, Expand, Compile

We never write unverified code. Every workflow and agent prompt is vetted through the PAL framework.

P · Parse

Deconstruct Intent

Isolate business objectives, ICP constraints, and CRM data boundaries.

A · Ambiguity Scan

Eliminate Blindspots

Identify missing API webhooks, deduplication rules, and delivery failure states.

L · Latent Intent

Map Downstream Needs

Anticipate rep edge cases, CRM validation rules, and future tool migrations.

E · Expand

Draft Architecture

Write technical specifications, data schemas, and prompt recipes.

C · Compile

Deploy To Production

Deploy deterministic workflows into your production accounts with tests.
