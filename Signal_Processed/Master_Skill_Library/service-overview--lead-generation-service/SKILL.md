1. Project Overview
The Lead Generation Service is designed to help businesses build a robust pipeline of qualified prospects, ensuring a steady flow of high-potential leads for their sales teams. Revenabled utilizes advanced tools and techniques to identify and target ideal customers, generating lead lists tailored to your business objectives and sales strategy.
2. Scope of Work
Target Market Identification
: Define the ideal customer profile (ICP) and segment your target audience based on industry, company size, geography, and other relevant factors.
Lead Research & Data Collection
: Use advanced lead generation tools to gather contact information and company data for high-potential prospects.
Lead Qualification & Segmentation
: Ensure leads meet predefined criteria and are segmented for more effective outreach and conversion.
Data Integration
: Upload leads into your CRM or sales automation platform for seamless follow-up and nurturing.
Continuous Optimization
: Regularly refine and expand the lead generation process to increase lead quality and volume.
3. Deliverables
Detailed lead lists segmented by industry, company size, and geography.
Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) creation and market segmentation reports.
Lead data, including contact information and company details, delivered in a format compatible with your CRM or automation tool.
Ongoing analysis and refinement of lead quality.

Ongoing Lead Delivery
