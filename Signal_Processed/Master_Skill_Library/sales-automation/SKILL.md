Service Overview | Sales Automation Setup
Prepared By: Revenabled Sales Enablement Agency

1. Project Overview
This service overview outlines the comprehensive process of setting up sales automation for the
client. The goal is to streamline the client’s outreach, lead management, and sales process
through automation tools. Revenabled will assist in selecting the appropriate tools, configuring
automation sequences, and integrating multi-channel outreach efforts (email, phone, LinkedIn).

2. Scope of Work
● Sales Automation Tool Selection: Assist the client in selecting the right sales
automation tool based on their unique needs (e.g., Reply.io, Apollo.io, HubSpot Sales,
Streak, or Mixmax). A detailed recommendation will be provided, considering pricing and
features.
● Email Automation Setup: Set up and configure email sequences within the selected
tool, ensuring proper lead nurturing, follow-ups, and CRM integration for tracking. ●
Sequence Configuration & Lead Upload: Design and configure outreach sequences
tailored to the client’s ideal customer profile (ICP), with segmented leads uploaded into the
system.
● Multi-Channel Outreach Integration (LinkedIn, Phone, Email): Integrate phone and
LinkedIn DM outreach into the sequences to ensure consistent communication across
platforms.
● Messaging Templates: Provide customized email, phone, and social media (LinkedIn)
outreach templates for up to 4 target audiences based on the client's sales segments.

3. Deliverables
● Sales Automation Tool Selection Report: A comprehensive recommendation on the
most suitable sales automation tool.
● Configured Sales Automation Tool: Complete setup and configuration of selected
tools with full CRM integration.
● Custom Email Sequences: Tailored email sequences configured for different audience
segments.
● Multi-Channel Outreach Integration: Setup of LinkedIn, phone, and email scripts
within the sequences.
● Messaging Templates: Custom email, phone, and DM templates for up to 4 target
audiences.
