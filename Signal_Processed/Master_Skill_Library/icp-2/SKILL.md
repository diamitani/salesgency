## Knowledge Source

ICP 1 – Venture-Backed SaaS Startups (Seed → Series B)
Industry
: B2B SaaS (AI, HR Tech, MarTech, FinTech, Productivity Tools)
 
Company Size
: 10–150 employees | $1M–$20M ARR
 
Geography
: North America & Europe (English-first markets)
 
Department / Buyer Role
: Founder/CEO, Head of Growth, VP of Sales, RevOps Lead
Pain Points
Struggling to build a predictable outbound motion without a full in-house team
Founders or junior SDRs juggling outreach with limited consistency
No CRM or poor setup (HubSpot/Pipedrive underutilized)
Messaging unproven, lacking ICP clarity
Pressure from investors to ramp pipeline and shorten sales cycles
Goals & KPIs
Hit aggressive pipeline targets to support funding milestones
Shorten sales cycle with better sales assets & playbooks
Achieve 20–30%+ reply rates and steady meeting flow
Stand up repeatable, founder-independent outbound
Buying Triggers
Recent funding round
Hiring their first or second sales rep
Missed last quarter’s pipeline goals
Evaluating CRM migration or revamp
Buying Objections
Fear of “outsourced agency” not understanding product deeply
Budget sensitivity at early stages
Desire to “build in-house”
Preferred Channels / Tone
Email & LinkedIn outreach, founder-to-founder tone
Case studies from similar-stage SaaS companies
Conversational, ROI-focused messaging
ICP 2 – Mid-Market Services Firms (Consulting, Marketing, IT, Agencies)
Industry
: Professional Services, IT Consulting, B2B Agencies
 
Company Size
: 50–500 employees | $10M–$50M revenue
 
Geography
: US, UK, Canada, ANZ
 
Department / Buyer Role
: CRO, VP Sales, Head of Business Development, COO
Pain Points
Heavy reliance on referrals, lumpy pipeline
Existing SDR teams underperforming, high turnover
CRM chaos: missing data, poor reporting
Limited personalization in outbound → low reply rates
Long sales cycles without consistent deal progression
Goals & KPIs
Diversify beyond referrals, build outbound at scale
Reduce SDR cost per meeting booked
Increase win rates with stronger playbooks + sales assets
Get visibility into pipeline health & forecast accuracy
Buying Triggers
Plateaued growth from referrals/word-of-mouth
Scaling into new verticals/geographies
Recent head of sales hire pushing for outbound
Missed revenue targets for 2+ quarters
Buying Objections
Concern about quality of leads from outbound
Preference for inbound-heavy GTM
IT/security concerns with domain setup
Preferred Channels / Tone
Email + phone sequences, professional and authoritative tone
Industry-specific proof points
ROI framing: cost of SDR vs. cost of RevEnabled
ICP 3 – Growth-Stage Tech Scaleups (Series C+ / Pre-IPO)
Industry
: SaaS / Tech-enabled B2B Services
 
Company Size
: 200–2,000 employees | $50M–$500M ARR
 
Geography
: North America, EMEA
 
Department / Buyer Role
: VP Sales Ops, RevOps Director, GTM Systems Leader
Pain Points
Outbound program exists but inconsistent across regions/teams
Tech stack complexity (multiple CRMs, automation tools)
Sales enablement scattered across departments
Reps underperform due to lack of unified playbooks and assets
Pressure to scale pipeline efficiency pre-IPO
Goals & KPIs
Standardize global outbound motion
Improve SDR productivity (meetings booked per rep/month)
Scale automation while maintaining personalization
Mature RevOps with reporting clarity
Buying Triggers
Expansion into new international markets
Preparing for IPO or acquisition
SDR team hitting diminishing returns
Desire to consolidate tech stack
Buying Objections
Preference to build/keep everything in-house
Concerns over integrating with complex RevOps infrastructure
Perception of RevEnabled as “startup-focused” vs. enterprise-ready
Preferred Channels / Tone
Strategic workshops, LinkedIn thought leadership, email nurturing
Data-driven proof points
Executive-level messaging on scalability and governance
