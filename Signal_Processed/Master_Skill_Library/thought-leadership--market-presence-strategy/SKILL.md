Private "AI" Podcast
Private "AI" Podcast Strategy | Lead Generation & Thought Leadership
Concept & Positioning
Private "AI"
 (a play on "Private Eye") is a Scalytics-branded podcast dedicated to investigating and exploring the biggest challenges in 
enterprise AI, data privacy, federated learning, and compliance
.
The name instantly signals:
Privacy-first approach.
Technical and compliance rigor.
"Investigative" / consultant tone → 
We're the AI detectives solving big challenges.
Podcast Structure
Host: Sales Engineer (primary), rotating guests from leadership (TAM, SIA) and external industry experts.
Guests: AI leaders (CTOs, Heads of AI/ML), Compliance officers, and clients.
Format: Interview-style, with occasional "investigative report" solo episodes.
Length: 20–30 mins per episode.
How It Drives Lead Generation
Cold Outreach Hook
When OEM/IEM contacts prospects, the intro is:
"We’re researching how [Industry] leaders are handling AI and compliance challenges for an episode of our Private AI podcast. Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation or even guesting on the show?"
Why it works:
Removes sales pressure.
Puts the prospect in a position of authority.
Starts a discovery conversation without a pitch.
Content Creation → Warm Follow-Up
Even if they don’t buy immediately, guests and contributors become warm leads. Post-episode follow-up is natural.
Brand Authority
Regular episodes position Scalytics as the 
go-to authority
 on compliance, federated AI, and enterprise model orchestration.
Event Tie-Ins
 When attending conferences (CES, AWS, Meta, AI Act events), the podcast can do 
live recordings or interviews
 to draw traffic and further extend reach.
Key Benefits
Lead Gen:
 Turns cold outreach into warm discussions.
Account Access:
 Opens doors at target companies under the guise of "market research," not sales.
Thought Leadership:
 Positions Scalytics as both a vendor and an industry educator.
Content Engine:
 Generates evergreen marketing content.
SE Development:
 Sales Engineers build public authority and deepen expertise.
