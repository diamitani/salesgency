Product & Solution Overview
Scalytics Connect Platform
 is the cornerstone of our offering.
Key Capabilities:
Run private AI models at scale
 — for high-security, enterprise-grade applications.
Federated Learning:
 Decentralized AI that allows training models without moving data across locations or jurisdictions.
Model Context Protocol (MCP):
 Enables private semantic search and multimodal AI capabilities tailored for enterprise needs.
Open Model Orchestration:
 Supports leading models including Meta’s LLaMa, Mistral, Gemma, and integrates seamlessly with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini.
Deployment Flexibility:
 Cloud-native (Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform) or on-premises deployments.

Fully managed service.
Open ecosystem avoiding vendor lock-in.
Compliance-ready: General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), and the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act).

Sales Process & Methodology
Sales Process Stages:
Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) → Sales Qualified Lead (SQL) → Sales Accepted Opportunity (SAO) → Demo → Proposal → Close → Customer Success.
Qualification Frameworks:
Inbound:
 Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline (BANT).
Outbound:
 Problem, Authority, Consequences, Target Profile, Timing (PACTT) and Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion (MEDDIC).
Sales Methodology:
The Challenger Sale
 — Teach, Tailor, Take Control.
Our process prioritizes education and value-driven conversations rather than product pitching.
Outreach & Messaging Framework
Outbound Email Sequence:
A five-touch sequence combining email, LinkedIn messages, and phone calls. Messaging emphasizes privacy, compliance outcomes, and measurable return on investment (ROI).
LinkedIn Messaging Flow:
Connection request → Share value proposition → Offer relevant case study → Request discovery meeting.
Cold Call Talk Track:
Open by addressing compliance and privacy pain points.
Share quick success stories from similar customers.
Ask a discovery question → Call to Action (CTA) for an Account Executive (AE) meeting.
Objection Handling Examples:
"We have internal AI infrastructure."
 → Scalytics complements existing solutions and accelerates outcomes.
"Vendor lock-in concerns."
 → Scalytics provides open models and flexible Application Programming Interfaces (APIs).
Sales Enablement Tools
Available Resources:
Battlecards:
 Objection handling and competitor positioning.

 Core value proposition and proof points.

 Email and LinkedIn cadences tailored to ICP pain points.

Compensation Plans
Role-Based Compensation Overview:
Sales Development Representatives (SDRs):
 Bonuses for SQL generation and conversion accelerators for SAOs.
Account Executives (AEs):
 10–15% commission on Annual Contract Value (ACV), plus accelerators for multi-year deals.
Customer Success Managers (CSMs):
 Renewal and upsell bonuses tied to NRR targets.
Team Structure
POD Model:
Each team POD is built for technical consultative selling and seamless customer success. PODs are aligned by industry vertical or geographic region.
Roles & Responsibilities:
Outbound Engagement Manager (OEM):
 Identifies, researches, and contacts target accounts. Responsible for outbound prospecting and booking qualified meetings.
Inbound Engagement Manager (IEM):
 Manages and qualifies inbound leads, ensuring rapid response and conversion to discovery meetings.
Sales Engineer (SE):
 Leads technical discovery, delivers demos, handles objection management, and collaborates with the SIA on solution design.
Solution Implementation Architect (SIA):
 Designs the technical solution post-demo and leads implementation to ensure successful deployment.
Technical Account Manager (TAM):
 Oversees post-sale relationship, ensuring adoption, performance, compliance success, renewals, and upsell opportunities.
Leadership Hierarchy: 
Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) → Sales Enablement → POD Leads → OEM/IEM/SE/SIA/TAM Team Members.
Metrics & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Funnel Stage KPIs:
Top Funnel:
 Conversion rate from Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs) to Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs).
Mid Funnel:
 SQL to Sales Accepted Opportunity (SAO) conversion.
Close:
 Win Rate, Deal Velocity, and Average Contract Value (ACV).
Post-Sale:
 Net Revenue Retention (NRR), Upsell Rate.
Activity Metrics by Role:
OEM/IEM Activity:
 Conversations initiated, meetings booked, reply-to-demo conversion rates.
Sales Engineer (SE) Metrics:
 Technical discovery calls conducted, demos delivered, demo-to-deal conversion rate.
Solution Implementation Architect (SIA) Metrics:
 Deployment timelines, implementation success rate, technical escalations resolved.
Technical Account Manager (TAM) Metrics:
 Upsell rate, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) scores, renewals.
Customer Success & Expansion
Customer Handoff Template:
At deal closure, a detailed handoff occurs from the Sales Engineer (SE) and Solution Implementation Architect (SIA) to the Technical Account Manager (TAM), preserving all relationship context, technical requirements, and strategic growth opportunities.
Success Metrics:
Client Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are aligned to compliance adherence, AI deployment success, and adoption of Scalytics features (e.g., MCP usage, federated learning deployments).
Expansion Opportunities:
The TAM identifies and leads upsell/cross-sell discussions for Model Context Protocol (MCP) add-ons, advanced model orchestration services, and future compliance framework enhancements as the client’s needs evolve.
