The purpose of this guide is to direct any owner, executive, or team member of an organization on how to set up and implement a sales operations strategy and technical stack to empower revenue enablement.
The steps involved are:
Creating a sales playbook. 
Develop your action plan, company thesis and product value proposition. 
Identify your core customers, opportunities and the competitive landscape. 
Draft processes, messaging and internal/external documents.
Generate target, priority and secondary leads
Set up or integrate a lead generation system (tools, APIs, scraping).
Download contact information for your Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs).
Upload to your chosen CRM and enable AI to create research insights.
Configuring your email domain (optional)
Each mailing url (
yourname@yourcompany.com
) has a unique footprint that gets tracked across the web. If it’s brand new and starts activating immediately, the system will detect your activity and limit you from outreach if your messages are not well received. Even worse, if it is established, you don’t want to risk shut down of your very lifeblood and existence. It is important to set up a similar domain for outreach, and/or gradually increase your limits for sending to cold parties (those not aware that you are contacting them - sales copy). To do this:
Purchase a new domain that matches your existing, but with a slight change (.io, or .co). Alternatively, you can use a sub domain (your 
name@sales.company.com
).
Configure DNS records (DMARC, DKIM, SPF) to meet standards and your platform’s settings (SMTP, IPOP, etc). Don’t worry, these are simple and non-changing factors that you will learn to grow.
Warm up your email address. Purchase a service or manually send emails (around 50 a day if you can) to people or inboxes that you know will reply (say your family and friends, or other accounts that you know). Your provider will see this and give you a score of your value to others.
Configure your Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform
Set up or integrate an existing CRM tool
Configure Contact Types and Properties
Configure Account Types and Properties
Configure Transaction Types and Properties
Configure Sales Funnel and Steps
Configure File Structure and Properties
Integrate email to enable outreach tracking
Configure your Automation Platform
Set up or integrate existing email automation tool
Configure outreach sequence and channels
Create reporting system and tracking insights
And there you have it! With these steps you should be ready to set up and maintain a sales operations system!

Configure DNS records (DMARC, DKIM, SPF) to ensure deliverability.

Select and set up a CRM tool, or integrate with your existing subscription.

Integrate email, import contacts and upload files (assets, templates, etc).

Select and set up an outreach automation system or integrate existing. 
Define core channels (email, phone, etc) and configure sequence steps.
Integrate with CRM and customize tracking and reporting dashboard.
