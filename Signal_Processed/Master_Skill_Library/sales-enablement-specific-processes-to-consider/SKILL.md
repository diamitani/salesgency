Sales Enablement-Specific Processes to Consider
In addition to the legal contracts, here are other key processes to develop and include in your sales documentation:
Sales Playbook
: A detailed guide for your internal sales teams and reps on how to manage the outreach process, handle objections, and use sales tools.
Client Success Plan
: A detailed document showing how you will onboard clients, manage their campaigns, and track success.
Lead Qualification Checklist
: A document outlining the criteria for leads to be considered sales-qualified.
Training Manuals
: Training documentation for new employees or clients on how to use CRM tools, email platforms, or lead management systems.
Next Steps for Drafting and Implementation:
