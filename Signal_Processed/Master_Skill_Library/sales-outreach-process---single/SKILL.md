Step 1: Set Up Domain
Choose between the original company domain or a new one for separation.
Ensure DNS records (DMARC, DKIM, SPF) are correctly configured.
Step 2: Warm Up Domain
Use email warmup services to prevent email outreach from being marked as spam.
Step 3: Set Up Email Automation Tool
Select and configure an email automation tool (HubSpot, Reply.io, Apollo, etc.).
Create email accounts and integrate them into the tool.
Step 4: Set Up Email Sequences
Draft and input messaging sequences based on target markets.
Upload leads to the CRM and sync them with your outreach tool for tracking.
Step 5: Monitor and Adjust
Track email activity (opens, clicks, replies) and adjust messaging, sequences, and target audiences accordingly.
Respond promptly to replies and handle objections.
