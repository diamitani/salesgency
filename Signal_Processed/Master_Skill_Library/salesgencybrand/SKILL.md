## Who we are

SalesGency is an AI revenue automation agency (d/b/a of Diamitani Industries, led by Patrick Diamitani). We design and install AI revenue workflows inside a client's existing stack. Category phrase: **AI revenue workflows**. Tagline: **AI revenue workflows for modern GTM teams.**

One-line positioning: *SalesGency installs AI revenue workflows inside your existing stack, so every lead gets worked, every conversation becomes usable data, and every next step moves forward.*

Message order on every page: **Outcome, then Mechanism, then Proof.**

## Voice rules

1. Lead with the business result, not the model, framework or vendor list.
2. Say "installed in your stack." Never "all-in-one platform," "replace your stack" or "next-gen."
3. Say "AI revenue workflows." Use "agents" only when describing one specific workflow.
4. One primary call to action everywhere: **Get a GTM teardown.**
5. Human control is a feature: "human review," "approval points." Never "fully autonomous" or "hands-off."
6. Never invent metrics, customer logos, testimonials or results. Proof is added only when it can be verified.
7. Keep long vendor lists off the homepage. Name only systems we have implemented.
8. Sentence case. Plain, direct, calm. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype ("supercharge," "revolutionize," "game-changing").
9. Legal d/b/a line ("SalesGency is a d/b/a of Diamitani Industries.") appears in footers and legal pages only, never in a hero or bio.

## Visual identity

**Logo:** Outfit Bold wordmark "SalesGency" followed by a cyan dot. Files in `logos/`.

- Primary (light backgrounds): ink wordmark + cyan dot. `salesgency-wordmark-primary.svg`
- Reversed (dark backgrounds): white wordmark + cyan dot. `salesgency-wordmark-reversed.svg`
- Signature gradient (dark heroes only, one per screen): white "Sales", cyan-to-indigo "Gency". `salesgency-wordmark-gradient.svg`
- Mark (avatars, favicons): S + dot in a rounded square. `salesgency-mark-ink.svg`, `salesgency-mark-light.svg`
- Clear space: the height of the capital S on all sides. Minimum width 96px screen / 24mm print; mark 24px.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, add shadows or outlines, drop the dot, or use the gradient on a light ground.

**Color** (hex, use exactly):

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0A1428` | Wordmark, headlines, dark surfaces |
| Ink 2 | `#111E38` | Raised dark surface |
| Mist | `#F4F9FC` | Default page ground |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on Mist |
| Signal Cyan | `#00C2FF` | The dot; accents on dark. Never text on a light ground. |
| Action Blue | `#0369A1` | Links, small blue text on light (5.6:1 on Mist) |
| Indigo | `#7C3AED` | Gradient end, rare emphasis |
| Slate | `#475569` | Body copy on light |
| Muted | `#566579` | Captions, labels |
| Line | `#DCE6EE` | Hairlines, card borders |

Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #00C2FF, #7C3AED)`, dark surfaces only, one per view, never behind body text.
Proportion: Mist 60%, Ink 20%, Slate 12%, Action 6%, Cyan 2%.

**Type:** Outfit (600, 700, 800) for headlines, prices and buttons, set tight (-2% to -3% tracking). Inter (400, 500, 600) for body, tables and captions. Labels: Inter 600, 12px caps, +12% tracking. Tabular numbers for prices. About 65 characters per line.

**Components:**

- Primary button: solid Ink, white text, small cyan dot at the end. On dark: white button, Ink text. Secondary: 1.5px outline. 10px radius, 44px minimum height. One primary per view.
- Cards: white on Mist, 1px Line border, 14 to 16px radius, no shadow. Only the recommended offer is lifted (2px Ink border).
- Bullets and status markers: the cyan dot. H2 headings on brand pages end with a cyan full stop.
- Icons: simple 1.5px line icons. No emoji as icons or bullets.
- Imagery: real workflow diagrams and real product screenshots. No stock handshakes, robots or glowing brains.
