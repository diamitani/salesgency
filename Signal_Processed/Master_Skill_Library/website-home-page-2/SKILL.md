What's included in the setup fee?

Why is there a 3-month minimum engagement?
Outbound sales is a process that requires time to optimize. The 3-month minimum allows us to properly warm up your domain, test and refine messaging, and generate meaningful results. Most clients see the best ROI after 2-3 months of optimization.
Can I customize a package?

Frequently Asked Questions
Why do I need a separate domain for outreach?
Using a separate domain for cold outreach protects your main domain's reputation. If your outreach campaigns trigger spam filters, your main domain remains unaffected for transactional and relationship emails.
How long does the warmup process take?
A proper email warmup typically takes 2-4 weeks. We gradually increase sending volume and monitor engagement metrics to establish a positive sender reputation.
What email warmup tools do you use?
We use a combination of specialized warmup services like Warmup Inbox and strategic manual outreach to real contacts to establish authentic engagement patterns.
