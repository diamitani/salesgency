Business Plan: Revenabled Sales Enablement Platform
Executive Summary:
 Revenabled is a Sales Enablement Agency that deploys AI-powered sales infrastructure for startups, SMBs, and growth-stage companies. It offers CRM integration, sales automation, lead generation, email deliverability optimization, and AI agent deployment to create repeatable and scalable revenue systems. The goal is to make RevEnabled the go-to agency and platform for building modern sales stacks.
Business Model:
Target Customers:
 B2B companies, SaaS startups, consultants, service agencies
Revenue Streams:
One-time setup packages (CRM, email, AI)
Monthly retainers for automation, lead gen, and GPT optimization
Custom AI Agent builds ($2.5K+ per bot)
Fractional Head of Sales ($5K/mo)
Channels:
 Website (Softr or WordPress), LinkedIn, email campaigns, Google Ads
Customer Relationships:
 Personalized onboarding, client portals, AI-generated reports, ongoing check-ins
Key Partners:
 OpenAI, HubSpot, Zoho, Apollo, HeyGen, Make.com, Vercel
Key Resources:
 Internal CRM/automation team, AI engineers, outbound team, agency dashboard

Step-by-Step Instructions to Build the Platform:
Step 1: Domain and Hosting
Buy domain on Vercel or GoDaddy (e.g., revenabled.ai)
Set up hosting with Vercel or WordPress + Elementor
Step 2: CRM & Email Setup
Pick CRM (HubSpot Free or Zoho)
Setup email domain via Route53 or Resend
Configure DNS: SPF, DKIM, DMARC
Warmup using Mailwarm or Warmbox
Step 3: Website & Client Portal
Use Softr or WordPress + MemberPress
Create dashboards with Airtable views per client
Embed contracts, GPT chatbots, reports
Step 4: Automation & Outreach Tools
Integrate Make.com with CRM and Google Sheets
Create automation for:
Lead upload
Email send (Resend/Apollo)
Daily reporting via Chart.js or Sheets
Step 5: Custom GPT Deployment
Use OpenAI Custom GPT Builder or Assistants API
Setup agent with:
Intake form builder
Outreach sequencer
Meeting summary + AI insights
Embed assistant in portal or iframe
Step 6: Onboarding and Reports
Use Make or Zapier to:
Trigger onboarding messages
Send sales performance reports
Update Airtable based on replies
