# Prospect PAL

**Your agent copilot for building outbound sales motions.**

Prospect PAL helps sales teams book more qualified meetings. We give you an agent copilot tailored to your tech stack, product, and ICP, then equip it with skills, sub-agents, and a harness to build an outbound automation workflow in n8n, Make, Gumloop, or a custom AI solution that deploys from your instance.

---

## How it works

### 1. Choose your automation platform

Tell us where the motion should run: n8n, Make, Gumloop, or a custom build. We load the right language, setup, and documentation so the agent builds against your platform — not a generic template.

### 2. Add your knowledge base

Upload files about your company, ideal customer profile, and value proposition. Product or service pricing and sales-cycle notes are appreciated but not required. Messaging scripts help the agent keep or update your copy.

If you need help constructing these, the agent form walks you through building them out.

### 3. Identify your tools

List the integrations you already have in your automation service — or the ones you want in a custom build. You configure credentials in your own settings. Keys never leave your instance.

## What you get

The agent compiles your inputs into a customized JSON file, tailored to your data and based on proven workflow templates. Upload it to your workspace, add your credentials, and go.

If you connect your account, our AI can generate the workflow directly on your platform.

Each of these tools lets us build a customizable outreach workflow with a deterministic process. Bring your own key and automate unlimited sales campaigns with the master agent, canvas, and workspace.

## The tools we use

| Tool | Role |
|---|---|
| **n8n Engineer** | Master at building n8n workflows. Understands nodes, ELT, connectors, and platform docs. Common integrations include HubSpot, Clay, Amplemarket, Apollo, and more. |
| **Make & Gumloop Engineer** | Same capability, specialized for Make.com and Gumloop. |
| **n8n Execution Agent** | Analyzes workflows by execution run and ID. Scans for failures and suggests fixes. |
| **Daily Execution Report** | Sends a daily update of workflow runs: nodes/modules processed, data packs stored or deleted, leads processed, leads emailed, open/reply rates (if connected to an outreach tool), and more. Keeps you on track of what is happening in your flows. |
| **Prospect Automation Workflow** | The base workflow. Foundational n8n / Make / Gumloop flow that connects each part based on structure and campaign setup. Multiple guides to select. |
| **Custom Build Prompt** | System instructions to generate a custom n8n, Make, or Gumloop prospect automation workflow from your inputs and the automation template. The essential skill. Use it with the automation engineer to edit. |
