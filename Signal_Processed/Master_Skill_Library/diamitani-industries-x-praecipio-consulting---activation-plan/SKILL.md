Overview
About the Engagement
Praecipio Consulting is currently reviewing Patrick Diamitani for an internal GTM role, or for a Forward Deployed AI Engineer opportunity.
This plan proposes an alternative: a six-month consulting activation, with the option to renew, in which Patrick consults, strategizes, and executes on a range of internal go-to-market efforts for Praecipio Consulting — without the overhead of a full-time hire, and with a scope built specifically around Praecipio's tools, team, and pipeline.
02  
Scope of Engagement
Core Deliverables
Six systems form the core of this engagement. Each combines a piece of Praecipio's existing stack with a purpose-built AI workflow.
Prospect Automation Workflow
 — Combine Praecipio's technical stack with its Ideal Customer Profiles to build a system that generates leads, conducts research, and sends personalized outbound messages on behalf of sales reps.
Inbound Automation Workflow
 — Combine the CRM and the website's contact submission form with a system that classifies every inbound message, correctly assigns MQLs to the right internal team, and increases lead-to-response speed.
Pre-Call Workflow
 — Combine upcoming external meetings with prospect research to automatically generate a briefing report and deliver it to the team member taking the call.
Post-Call Workflow
 — Combine past meetings with call transcript research to analyze outcomes and automatically execute the appropriate follow-up message or task.
Daily Execution Report
 — Combine daily runs across all active workflows into a system that sends a spreadsheet and summary of any failed automations.
Go-To-Market Dashboard
 — Pull data from Praecipio's platforms and systems, combined with agreed-upon KPIs and metrics, into a single dashboard that visualizes GTM performance.
Ad Hoc Support
Beyond the six core systems, Patrick will construct and improve ad hoc workflows, skills, and agents as needed throughout the engagement — responding to whatever the GTM team's priorities require, month to month.
03  
Optional Expansion Projects
Available as extensions to the core engagement, scoped and priced separately as priorities emerge:
Custom SDR Agent
 — Connect Claude models to a chatbot interface, giving the team direct, conversational access to GTM activities: calling, lead generation, outreach, and research.
Sales Enablement Agent
 — Connect and configure the broader tech stack into a single, optimized, AI-native system that gives every employee a consistent approach to their workflow.
GTM Center of Excellence
 — Build a library of internal skills and workflows, paired with AI-generated tutorial and curriculum videos, to train the team on each tool.
