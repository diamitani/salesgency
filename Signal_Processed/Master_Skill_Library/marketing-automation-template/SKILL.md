OVERVIEW:
The purpose of this document is to outline an email marketing strategy that will enable your
firm to maximize responses from key targets and provide growth in the US market.
SEGMENTATION
Creating segments for each industry sector helps to personalize outreach based on the needs of
the individual company or market. For example, the financial services industry (investment
banks, hedge funds), Advertising agencies, venture backed startups (Series A +) and small
businesses may all have a need for your product/service but different budgets and use cases.

LEAD GENERATION:
It is important to identify the core departments and decision makers required to engage with
when it comes to lead generation. This includes Directors and VPs, as well as COO’s and CEOs
for startups and SMEs. These leads can be purchased through databases, scraped using web
crawling tools and manually found online via Linkedin and company websites.
OUTREACH SEQUENCING:
When contacting prospects, an email sequence may contain 5-7 messages spread out over the
course of 1-2 months. Upon response, a new follow-up sequence can start in the CRM to
maintain a level of conversation and interest. To recycle leads that did not open or reply to
emails, direct messaging using LinkedIn is another option to increase conversion.
POTENTIAL SUBJECT LINES
Creating an engaging subject line can substantially increase open rates and thus boost reply and
even close statistics. Choose wording that reflects an open-ended outcome for the reader.
