## Knowledge Source

Sales Process Summary
For the agency's sales process and workflow, here’s an optimized breakdown:
Initial Client Onboarding
:
Discuss client goals, needs, and timelines.
Present package options and provide a quote.
Sign an MSA and initiate project planning.
Domain & Outreach Setup
:
Set up domain, warm it up, and configure DNS settings for the outreach campaign.
Begin structuring the CRM, lead generation, and automation tools.
Email Sequence Development
:
Draft customized email sequences for the client’s target audience.
Integrate the CRM with an outreach tool (Reply.io, HubSpot) to track engagement.
Outreach Execution
:
Launch the outreach campaigns.
Track campaign results (opens, clicks, responses).
Adjust messaging and targets accordingly.
Data Reporting & Analysis
:
Provide the client with regular reports on campaign performance.
Optimize based on responses, data feedback, and lead activity.
