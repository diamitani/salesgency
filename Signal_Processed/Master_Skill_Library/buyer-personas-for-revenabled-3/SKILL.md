Buyer Personas for RevEnabled
1. The Scrappy SaaS Founder
(ICP 1 – Venture-Backed SaaS Startups, Seed → Series B | Essentials Fit)
Title / Role(s):
 Founder/CEO, Head of Growth, RevOps Lead (early stage)
 
Department / Function:
 Leadership, GTM, Sales Ops (nascent)
 
Company Profile:
B2B SaaS (AI, HRTech, MarTech, FinTech, Productivity)
10–150 employees | $1M–$20M ARR
Based in NA/EU (English-first markets)
Key Goals / KPIs:
Hit aggressive pipeline targets to unlock next round of funding
Shorten sales cycle with better assets + playbooks
Build a repeatable outbound motion that doesn’t depend on the founder
Achieve 20–30%+ reply rates with steady meeting flow
Daily Responsibilities / Workflow Context:
Balancing fundraising, product roadmap, and early GTM
Overseeing first AE/SDR hires
Testing outbound messaging while juggling investor pressure
Pain Points & Challenges:
No dedicated outbound team, founder or junior SDRs doing outreach
CRM either missing or underutilized (HubSpot/Pipedrive)
Messaging unproven, ICP not crystalized
Pressure from investors to show pipeline predictability
Buying Triggers:
Just raised funding round
Hiring first/second sales rep
Missed last quarter’s pipeline targets
Considering CRM revamp/migration
Objections & Risks:
Skepticism that an external partner won’t “get” their product
Early-stage budget sensitivity
Desire to “build in-house”
Preferred Channels / Tone:
Founder-to-founder tone, LinkedIn + Email
Conversational, ROI-focused messaging
Case studies from similar-stage SaaS
Decision Role:
 Founder = Champion + Decision-maker
Buying Behavior:
 Fast, decisive buyers when they see founder credibility and fast ROI
2. The Pipeline-Plateaued Sales Leader
(ICP 2 – Mid-Market Services Firms | Professional Fit)
Title / Role(s):
 CRO, VP of Sales, Head of Biz Dev, COO
 
Department / Function:
 Sales, Business Development, Operations
 
Company Profile:
Professional Services, IT Consulting, Marketing Agencies
50–500 employees | $10M–$50M revenue
NA/UK/Canada/ANZ
Key Goals / KPIs:
Diversify pipeline beyond referrals/inbound
Reduce SDR cost per meeting booked
Strengthen win rates with assets + playbooks
Gain visibility into pipeline health and forecast accuracy
Daily Responsibilities / Workflow Context:
Managing SDR/BDR teams, often with high turnover
Reviewing pipeline forecasts and campaign ROI
Coordinating outbound strategy across verticals
Pain Points & Challenges:
Over-reliance on referrals, leading to lumpy pipeline
Existing SDRs underperform, costly churn
CRM chaos: poor reporting, incomplete data
Limited personalization in outreach → low reply rates
Long sales cycles without progression
Buying Triggers:
Plateaued growth from referrals
New vertical or geo expansion
New head of sales pushing outbound
Missed revenue targets 2+ quarters
Objections & Risks:
Doubt around outbound lead quality
Preference for inbound-heavy GTM
IT/security concerns with domain setup
Preferred Channels / Tone:
Email + Phone sequences
Professional, authoritative tone
ROI framing: cost per SDR vs. cost of RevEnabled
Decision Role:
 CRO/COO = Final decision-maker; VP Sales often champion
Buying Behavior:
 Want proven frameworks + predictable ROI before scaling commitment
3. The Scale-Minded RevOps Leader
(ICP 3 – Growth-Stage Scaleups, Series C+ / Pre-IPO | Enterprise Fit)
Title / Role(s):
 VP Sales Ops, RevOps Director, GTM Systems Leader
 
Department / Function:
 Revenue Operations, GTM Systems, Sales Enablement
 
Company Profile:
SaaS / Tech-enabled B2B Services
200–2,000 employees | $50M–$500M ARR
NA + EMEA
Key Goals / KPIs:
Standardize outbound globally across regions/teams
Improve SDR productivity (meetings booked/rep/month)
Scale automation while preserving personalization
Mature RevOps → reporting clarity & predictability
Daily Responsibilities / Workflow Context:
Overseeing GTM systems across global orgs
Consolidating CRM/automation stack
Managing enablement content + process rollouts
Preparing exec team for IPO-level efficiency
Pain Points & Challenges:
Inconsistent outbound execution across teams
Tech stack complexity (multiple CRMs/tools)
Scattered enablement across departments
Underperforming reps due to lack of unified playbooks
Buying Triggers:
Expanding into international markets
IPO or acquisition prep
SDR performance plateauing
Desire to consolidate RevOps stack
Objections & Risks:
Preference to build/keep in-house
Concerns about integration complexity
Fear RevEnabled is “too startup-focused”
Preferred Channels / Tone:
Strategic workshops, thought leadership, LinkedIn/email nurturing
Data-driven proof points, scalability framing
Executive-level messaging (governance, ROI at scale)
Decision Role:
 VP/Director RevOps = Champion; CRO often final sign-off
Buying Behavior:
 Longer cycle, enterprise diligence, need for scalability proof
