## The Brand at a Glance

**What SalesGency does:** Builds and manages AI-powered sales systems —
deal qualification, call intelligence, revenue attribution, and outbound
infrastructure — for B2B revenue teams, sold as done-for-you monthly
engagements ($5,000–$20,000/month).

**Tagline [DRAFT — confirm]:** "We don't pitch AI. We ship it."

**Target customer (ICP) [DRAFT — confirm]:** B2B companies with an active
sales team (roughly 10–50+ reps) running a CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce), who are
losing revenue to manual pre/post-call work, unscored inbound, unclear
attribution, or ungoverned AI/enrichment spend. Think: the buyer who has
already tried a point-solution SaaS tool and is still doing the real work by
hand.

**Primary CTA [DRAFT — confirm]:** "Get a free AI Ops Audit" — not "book a
demo." At this price point, buyers need evidence before a call, not a pitch.

**Why we're different:** Every system SalesGency sells was built and proven
inside a real production GTM organization first — not designed in a vacuum
and pitched as theory. Proof of work, not a deck of promises.

## Brand Personality

Every piece of content should reflect these traits:

**Proven** — We show receipts (real numbers, real outcomes), we don't just claim expertise.

**Direct** — Short sentences. No jargon stacking. Say the specific thing.

**Unimpressed by hype** — We're openly skeptical of "AI magic" language. We talk about systems, not sorcery.

**Operator-minded** — We talk like someone who has actually run the pipeline, not like a marketer describing it from outside.

**Confident, not boastful** — State results plainly. Let the number do the bragging, not the adjective.

**Summary:** We sound like an engineer who can also sell — credible, specific, and allergic to fluff.

## Tone of Voice

**Do:**
- Lead with a concrete number or outcome, not an adjective ("Recovered from 21-of-25 failing runs" not "dramatically improved reliability")
- Name the actual mechanism ("deterministic rules engine," "dry-run harness") — specificity builds trust with technical buyers
- Keep sentences short. Cut adverbs.
- Use "we" for SalesGency, never "I" in client-facing content (even though it's currently a team of one — the systems and process are what's being sold)
- Acknowledge tradeoffs and limits honestly when relevant — it builds more trust than a claim of perfection

**Don't:**
- Don't say "revolutionary," "game-changing," "cutting-edge," "unlock," "supercharge," "seamless," or "leverage" (as a verb)
- Don't use exclamation points in client-facing copy
- Don't pitch "AI" as the headline — the *outcome* is the headline, AI is the mechanism
- Don't inflate numbers — every stat used should be real and defensible under questioning
- Don't use stock-photo-style claims like "world-class" or "best-in-class" without a specific reason attached

## Visual Identity

**Wordmark:** "Sales" + "Gency" — two-tone lockup, "Sales" in dark navy, "Gency" in teal, set in a bold sans-serif (see bundled logo file).

**Color Palette:**

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (dark) | Deep Navy | `#0B1524` |
| Primary (dark, alt) | Navy Blue | `#0F1E33` |
| Accent | Teal | `#4FD1C5` |
| Accent (deep) | Dark Teal | `#0B8C7E` |
| Text (body, dark bg) | Cool Gray | `#B7C4D6` |
| Text (body, light bg) | Slate | `#45526A` |
| Background (light) | Off-white | `#FBFCFD` |
| Border/hairline | Light Gray | `#E3E8EF` |

**Usage:**
- Navy is the dominant brand color for headers, covers, and CTAs — it signals seriousness and enterprise credibility.
- Teal is the *proof* color — always pair it with a number or stat. Never use teal purely decoratively.
- Light backgrounds (`#FBFCFD`) for body/detail sections, navy for cover pages and closing CTAs.

**Typography:** Bold sans-serif (Helvetica Neue / Arial as fallback) for headers; clean, readable body text at 9.5–10.5pt for print documents.

**Layout patterns:**
- Stat blocks: dark navy card, large teal number, small gray label underneath
- Proof callouts: teal left-border, light teal-tinted background, dark bold title + gray body
- Offer/service cards: light background, hairline border, bold title + price aligned right, italicized "built from" proof line, pill-tag feature list

## Messaging Pillars (use these as building blocks)

1. **Production-proven, not demo-proven.** Every system has already survived real production failures and been fixed — that's the credibility no other AI agency at this price point can claim.
2. **Evidence before pitch.** The free AI Ops Audit finds real, specific problems in a prospect's own data before any sales conversation happens.
3. **Systems, not scripts.** Emphasize durability and reliability engineering (dry-run harnesses, idempotency, deterministic rules) over "AI did something clever."
4. **Honest metrics.** When describing attribution or results, use the same honesty-tiered framing (sourced / influenced / excluded) rather than inflated claims.

## Content Examples

**❌ Off-brand (hype-driven):**
> "Unlock the power of AI to revolutionize your sales pipeline! Our cutting-edge automation seamlessly supercharges your revenue engine."

**✅ On-brand (proof-driven):**
> "We built an AI qualification workflow that went from failing 21 of 25 runs to reliable, end-to-end delivery — grounded in real account data, not generic AI filler."

**❌ Off-brand (vague claim):**
> "We help companies grow faster with AI."

**✅ On-brand (specific, honest):**
> "We build the system that screens every inbound deal against your eligibility rules the moment it hits your CRM — before a rep wastes time on it."
