The Architecture

One engine. Five pipeline stages. Zero manual steps.

Every prospect that enters the system is validated, enriched, researched, and messaged with a custom, personalized email — without a single manual step.

01

Trigger & Ingest

Skill-to-webhook, a daily CRM/spreadsheet pull, or a fully autonomous agent that finds ICP-matched companies on its own.

02

CRM Dedupe Shield

Checks every prospect against your existing records before anything else happens — no deal collisions, ever.

03

Contact Reveal

Finds and verifies the right people when you only have a company, or enriches the contacts you already have.

04

AI Research & Copy

Pulls company data, news, and buying signals, then writes a personalized Problem-Agitate-Solve email.

05

Sequence Enrollment

Enrolls the contact in your outreach platform and hands off to your CRM — ready for a human to take over.

Zero hardcoded credentials — every secret lives in your .env or n8n credential store.
CRM Dedupe Shield always runs before any outreach action to prevent deal collision.
Human approval gate for high-impact sends, with a 1-click Slack confirm.
Full execution observability — every run logged with structured data and error paths.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need n8n? +
For the Custom Engagement and DIY Package, yes — n8n is the automation backbone. SalesGency can help you set up your n8n instance as part of the engagement. For the Custom Agent, SalesGency can host the environment for you.
What if I don't have any contact data? +
The Company Automation path is built for exactly this. You provide a list of target companies (or an ICP description), and the system finds, validates, and enriches contact information before reaching out.
Can I use my existing CRM? +
Yes. HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Pipedrive, Zoho, Monday, and ClickUp are all supported. The CRM Dedupe Shield always checks your existing records before any contact is touched.
What data tools do I need? +
The minimum viable stack is one enrichment tool (Clay, Apollo, or ZoomInfo) and one outreach platform. SalesGency can advise on the best combination based on your budget and ICP.
