Top 5 AI Headlines of the Day

China Amplifies Support for AI and Tech Innovation: China announced plans to enhance support for artificial intelligence (AI), science, and technological innovation. Emphasizing the need for more breakthroughs and self-reliance, the government document prepared for the National People's Congress outlines fostering future industries such as biomanufacturing, quantum technology, embodied AI, and 6G technology.

Google Introduces 'AI Mode' in Search: Google is enhancing its search engine by incorporating its advanced Gemini 2.0 AI model to handle complex queries in coding and math. AI-generated overviews will be prominently displayed, prioritizing them above traditional web links, now extended to teen users without extra sign-in.

Software Giants Develop Autonomous AI Agents: ServiceNow, Salesforce, SAP, and Intuit are developing AI agents to handle tasks autonomously and support employees in customer service, HR, IT, and marketing. Currently, AI agents handle 80% of customer interactions at ServiceNow, with humans managing the more complex 20%.

Physical Infrastructure Challenges Threaten U.S. AI Leadership: A new report from the Center for Strategic & International Studies (CSIS) highlights the physical infrastructure challenges that may hinder the United States' ability to maintain its lead in artificial intelligence (AI). The report estimates that by 2030, the U.S. may need up to 90 gigawatts of new data center energy.

NVIDIA CEO to Unveil AI Innovations at GTC 2025: NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang, along with industry visionaries, is set to unveil the latest advancements in AI at GTC 2025, featuring over 1,000 sessions that provide insights into the future of AI infrastructure, scientific computing, and robotics.
