A Simple Guide to Connecting AI to Your Business Tools

If you use tools like HubSpot or an email marketing platform, you have probably thought about how powerful it would be if an AI assistant could use them for you. For example, telling an AI to add a new contact to your CRM or send a welcome email.

Until now, making tools and AI talk to each other was complicated. It required custom code for every single AI platform. A new method, called the Model Context Protocol (MCP), changes that.

What is This New Method?

Think of MCP as a universal adapter for your software.

Imagine if every phone and laptop needed a completely different charger. It would be a mess. That’s how connecting to AI used to be. MCP is like USB-C for AI. You create one standard connection, and any AI that understands this standard can plug right in.

You just have to create one simple "connector" for your tool. Then, AI platforms like GPT, Claude, and others can instantly understand how to use it.

Why This Matters for Your Business

This approach lets you make your business "AI-ready" without needing a team of developers. You can:

  • Connect an AI assistant directly to your HubSpot data.

  • Allow your AI to manage your email lists and campaigns.

  • Set it up once, and it will work with many different AI systems in the future.

How to Connect an AI Assistant to HubSpot: Step-by-Step

Let's walk through how you would give an AI assistant the ability to perform tasks in your HubSpot CRM.

Step 1: Decide What Your AI Should Do First, make a list of the specific jobs you want to hand off to your AI. Be clear and simple.

  • Example Job 1: Look up a contact's information.

  • Example Job 2: Create a new lead from information you provide.

  • Example Job 3: Update a deal's status (for example, from "In Progress" to "Closed").

Step 2: Describe Each Job in Plain English For each job, write a simple sentence that describes the action and the information needed. This is how the AI will learn what to do.

  • For Job 1: "Find a contact in HubSpot using their email address."

  • For Job 2: "Create a new contact with a first name, last name, and email."

  • For Job 3: "Update a deal's stage in HubSpot."

Step 3: Use a Tool to Create the Connector You will use a simple no-code tool or a template to turn your list of plain-English jobs into a single connector file. This file acts as a rulebook that any compatible AI can read.

Step 4: Give the Connector to Your AI Assistant In the settings for your custom AI assistant, you will upload or link to this connector file. The AI will instantly read the file and know exactly what actions it can perform in HubSpot for you.

How to Connect an AI Assistant to Your Email Platform: Step-by-Step

The process is the same for your email automation tool (like Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, etc.).

Step 1: Decide What Your AI Should Do List the email-related jobs you want to automate.

  • Example Job 1: Add a new person to your weekly newsletter list.

  • Example Job 2: Remove a person from a specific marketing campaign.

  • Example Job 3: Send the "New Customer Welcome" email.

Step 2: Describe Each Job in Plain English Write a simple description for each email task.

  • For Job 1: "Add a subscriber to the 'Weekly Newsletter' list using their email."

  • For Job 2: "Unsubscribe a contact from the 'Q3 Promotion' list."

  • For Job 3: "Send the 'Welcome' email template to a new contact."

Step 3: Update Your Connector File You can add these new email jobs to the same connector file you made for HubSpot. This creates one central rulebook for your AI that covers multiple tools.

Step 4: Give the Updated Connector to Your AI Assistant Provide the updated file to your AI assistant. It will now know how to perform its new jobs in your email platform in addition to its HubSpot tasks.

What You Don’t Need to Worry About

With this method, you can get started without technical stress.

  • You do not need to know how to code.

  • You do not need to build complex systems.

  • You do not need to ask a developer for help every time a new AI comes out.

If your business software has buttons, forms, or workflows, this method helps your AI assistant press those buttons safely and intelligently.
