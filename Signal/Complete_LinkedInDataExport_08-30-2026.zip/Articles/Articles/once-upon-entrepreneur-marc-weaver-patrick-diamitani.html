Created on 2016-11-01 18:09

Marc is the Founder of Databasable, a certified Amazon Web Services (AWS) Solutions Architect and database administration consultancy. Marc spent over 15 years working as a database administrator for large investment banks in London, Sydney and New York supporting Microsoft SQL Server, SAP ASE, MySQL and Oracle.

Occupation: FOUNDER/CLOUD SOLUTIONS ARCHITECT

Tell us about your background. Where were you born/raised and what has influenced you towards the path you’re on?

Cambridge, UK. I think I'm influenced by a desire to achieve more and not just settle for the status quo in life. I've always been driven to improve my life and make things better/more interesting/different.

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you decided to pursue your path?

For years I'd been thinking about starting my own business, but the actual moment came when I lost my US visa (long story) and one of the options available to me was to form my own business and get a US visa on the back of that.

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you knew you had chosen the right path?

I'm still not sure this is the right path, I'm not sure I ever will! But what I do know is that I'm much happier and engaged working for myself than I ever was working for someone else. 

What are the most difficult lessons you’ve learned?​

Faking it till you make it. Having to push myself outside of my comfort zone to improve my business and myself is something I find very hard to do.

How is dealing with success different than dealing with other experiences?

When I consider myself successful I'll let you know! ;-)

If you’re having a bad day, how do you keep yourself up?

I just think about the fact that I'm not stuck in a cubicle in the corporate world and then I walk 5 minutes to the beach and jump on my paddleboard!

What constitutes a good day?

A good day is one when I'm fully engaged, outside of my comfort zone and working or dealing with new and interesting projects/people. I've found that since I started my own business the hours in the day absolutely fly by. I do not know where the day goes.

Who would you like to have coffee with most and why?

Jeff Bezos. I'm building my business around AWS and I'd love to pick his brain to find out where he's taking it next. Also I have some suggestions for him....

On a scale of 1-10, how important is (a) a team (b) a good idea ( c) a strategy (d) organization?

a) 10 b) 5 c) 6 d) 10

If Napoleon Hill was asking you the secret right now, what would you tell him?

Firstly I'd tell him that I have no idea who he is! Secondly after I've googled him, I'd tell him that I'll let him know when I find out.
