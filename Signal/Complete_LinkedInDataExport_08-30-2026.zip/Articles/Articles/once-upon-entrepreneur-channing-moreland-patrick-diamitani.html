Once Upon an Entrepreneur | Channing Moreland

Created on 2016-11-01 18:06

Published on 2016-11-01 18:09

Channing Moreland is the CEO and Co-Founder of EVAmore, a platform designed to help student groups and administrators book talent for events. The company took part in the Project Music Accelerator in Nashville, Tennessee and has secured partnerships with major entertainment industry talent agencies and record labels.

Occupation: Entrepreneur / Startup owner

Tell us about your background. Where were you born/raised and what has influenced you towards the path you’re on?

I was born in Oklahoma, raised in Boston. Both of those places influenced me because I was focused on classical music in Oklahoma and Jazz in Boston- it caused me to lean towards songwriting and the music industry. I moved to Nashville and dove into the music tech industry- I don't think I would have if I hadn't found a unique fusion of love for music.

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you decided to pursue your path?

When we started producing our own large scale events in Nashville completely on our own with major stages, sponsors, and vendors- it became very real that I could create anything I wanted to if I put in the work and time.

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you knew you had chosen the right path?

When we accepted investment in college for an idea we concocted during our freshman year. Taking someone else's money makes it very real, scary, yet reassuring.

What are the most difficult lessons you’ve learned?

At the end of the day, you are the only one thinking about your idea. No one else will ever care as much as you do. I always remind myself of this when hiring, it helps me put myself in their shoes and where they would fit best.

How is dealing with success different than dealing with other experiences?

It is the quickest flow/ transition of emotions I feel. Exhaustion, excitement, proud, angry, stressed, anxious, reset, try harder for the next success. 

If you’re having a bad day, how do you keep yourself up?

I make myself do a project that I have been procrastinating on. It is the absolute worst feeling getting myself to do it on a bad day, but when you finish and feel accomplished, it's not so much of a bad day anymore.

What constitutes a good day?

When you learned something about yourself, about one of your team members, about your customer, or about your business. And one of those should happen every day.

Who would you like to have coffee with most and why?

A potential customer/employee that thinks our company is the dumbest thing to exist. I want to know why.

On a scale of 1-10, how important is (a) a team (b) a good idea ( c) a strategy (d) organization?

a) 10 (b) 4 c (8) d (9)

If Napoleon Hill was asking you the secret right now, what would you tell him?

My secret, at least, is that I accept the worst outcome and think about it every day- I don't let it worry me- but I keep it there so I know where I will never let myself end. Always keep pushing, because why not when it's something you have created and believe in?
