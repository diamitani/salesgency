Hey All! Got a bed time story for you. A bit of a picture book if you will.

In this edition, we will be going over how to build a custom YouTube Data Extractor using Make.com. This tool goes into a YouTube Channel and gathers all of the video meta data (Title, description, URL, etc), so you can use it for whatever purposes later - think RSS feed aggregator to repost content, automation to repost/summarize, transcription for knowledge base, etc. Hope you can follow along!

YouTube Data Extractor

How To Build It:

1. Go to Make.com and open up your dashboard. Click on "Create New Scenario" on the top right or left side bar.

2. Add a "YouTube" Module and select "Watch Channel." This will find all video results from that Channel ID.

3. To get the Channel ID, select, "Channel ID Finder" and enter the Youtube handle of the account you wish to extract, (i.e., https://www.youtube.com/"@channelname").

This should get you the Channel ID.

Insert how many videos you wish to get data from (I choose as many as possible) and save.

4. Create a "Google Sheets" Module and "Add New Row."

For this you're going to want to have a spreadsheet template already set up, with the headers availableIn this case, we are going to track, "Video Title, Video Description, Video ID, Channel Title, Channel ID, Video Link, etc."

For the Video Link portion, we will use the Youtube Formula, which = https://www.youtube.com/watch?v={​{1.videoId}} where the videoid is taken from the YouTube data. We're going to add the rest of the column data to the spreadsheet as well.

5. Hit "Run once" on the bottom left side of the screen and you're all set!
