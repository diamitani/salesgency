A.I. News - March 11, 2025

Now, let's get started with the AI news of the day:

DOGE deploys custom chatbot to automate federal tasks

The US Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), led by Elon Musk, has introduced a custom chatbot called GSAi to automate tasks for 1,500 federal workers. Tailored for government use, GSAi can draft emails, summarize text, write code, and create talking points, supporting general tasks akin to tools like ChatGPT. - Read More

China's Manus AI partners with Alibaba's Qwen team in expansion bid

Chinese AI startup Manus AI announced a strategic partnership with Alibaba's Qwen AI team to enhance its rollout of what it claims is the world's first general AI agent. Unlike chatbots, this AI agent can independently execute tasks with minimal input. Launched just a week prior, Manus AI gained attention on Chinese social media, outperforming OpenAI's DeepResearch and drawing comparisons to DeepSeek, a cost-effective rival to OpenAI. - Read More

Hugging Face Partners with Cerebras to Give Developers Access to Industry’s Fastest AI Inference for Open-Source Models

Cerebras and Hugging Face announced a partnership integrating Cerebras Inference into the Hugging Face platform, providing over five million developers access to the world’s fastest AI inference for open-source models. Cerebras Inference, powered by the CS-3 system, runs popular models like Llama 3.3 70B at over 2,200 tokens per second—70 times faster than GPU-based solutions—outpacing models like OpenAI’s o3-mini in tasks requiring high-speed reasoning. - Read More

Amazon Bedrock announces general availability of multi-agent collaboration

Amazon Bedrock announced the general availability (GA) of its multi-agent collaboration feature, enabling developers to build and manage networks of AI agents that work together on complex, multi-step workflows. Initially previewed at re:Invent 2024, this capability has been adopted across industries like finance, healthcare, retail, and agriculture to enhance efficiency and automation. - Read More

Airbnb CBO: AI Will Enable a ‘Concierge in Your Pocket

Airbnb’s Chief Business Officer, Dave Stephenson, announced at the Human[x] conference in Las Vegas on March 10, 2025, that AI is a top priority to enhance guest and host experiences. The company aims to create a “concierge in your pocket” by embedding AI across its operations, personalizing travel recommendations via user "passports" (profiles) and leveraging its massive scale. - Read More
