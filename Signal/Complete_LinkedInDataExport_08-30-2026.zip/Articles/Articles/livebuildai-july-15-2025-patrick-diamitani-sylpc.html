Top 5 Headlines for Today

1. Trump announces $70 billion clean energy and AI infrastructure plan Speaking at Carnegie Mellon during the Pennsylvania Energy & Innovation Summit, President Trump called for a historic $70 billion investment aimed at clean energy and AI. The initiative focuses on ramping up data center capacity and backing private sector efforts to localize AI infrastructure. CoreWeave and Google’s AI ambitions are central to this push.

2. CoreWeave commits $6 billion to build massive AI campus in Pittsburgh CoreWeave, a rising star in cloud compute, revealed plans for a $6 billion AI-focused campus in Pennsylvania. The data center will start at 100 megawatts and expand to 300 megawatts, supporting next-gen AI workloads and creating hundreds of local jobs. It’s one of the largest private investments in the state’s history.

3. Nvidia resumes AI chip exports to China Following a policy shift, Nvidia is back in the Chinese market with its high-performance H20 chips. The move, backed by both government approval and market urgency, saw Nvidia stock jump nearly 5 percent in premarket trading. This helps fill a massive supply gap in China’s AI sector and reopens a critical trade lane.

4. Google Cloud and Westinghouse bring AI to nuclear reactor design In a forward-thinking partnership, Westinghouse Electric is collaborating with Google Cloud to use AI in reactor design and optimization. The move is meant to streamline new reactor builds and improve existing systems, positioning AI as a key player in the future of nuclear energy.

5. UT Austin engineers use AI to invent ultra-cooling materials University of Texas researchers developed a new class of materials using AI that radiate heat more efficiently than traditional options.
