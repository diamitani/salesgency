Signing Up for PayPal

First, set up a PayPal account if you don’t have one. Visit paypal.com and hit “Sign Up” in the corner. Choose “Personal” (unless you’re registering your business), enter your email, verify your phone, and link a bank or debit card for payments. PayPal runs through standard security steps—think SMS code and email confirmation—but most users start sending or receiving money within minutes.

Getting Perplexity Pro (and Comet) With PayPal
