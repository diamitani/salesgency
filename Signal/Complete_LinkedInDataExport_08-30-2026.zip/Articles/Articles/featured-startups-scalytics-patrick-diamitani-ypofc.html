Scalytics: The Enterprise Operating System for Private AI

As businesses rapidly adopt artificial intelligence, Scalytics stands out as a platform that redefines what it means to be "AI-ready" in a privacy-centric environment. Scalytics is a modular AI orchestration platform specifically designed for secure, scalable, and compliant enterprise AI.

Scalytics' core vision is straightforward: "Your AI. Your Rules. Your Privacy." With increasing global demand for decentralized and explainable AI systems, Scalytics provides companies with a robust set of tools to train and deploy private large language models (LLMs) across international borders. This is achieved without compromising control, data sovereignty, or adherence to regulations.

Key Differentiators

  • Model Context Protocol (MCP): This Kafka-native framework facilitates semantic search and AI agent orchestration directly within your existing infrastructure.

  • Federated Learning Engine: Enables privacy-preserving, cross-border model training that is fully compliant with GDPR, HIPAA, and the upcoming AI Act.

  • DSO-Agent Architecture: A novel microservice system for LLM orchestration, grounding, and trust scoring. It directly addresses hallucinations through "Librarian Checks" and iterative relevance feedback.

  • Modular, Vendor-Neutral Stack: Scalytics supports open models such as Llama, Mistral, and Gemma, and offers API integrations with leading platforms like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini.

Bridging Data Silos and Modern AI

Scalytics is currently utilized across diverse sectors including healthcare, defense, fintech, travel, and manufacturing. It effectively bridges the gap between complex data silos and contemporary AI use cases. Whether the need is to unify data lakes, enable federated analytics, or deploy regulatory-safe generative AI, Scalytics provides the necessary infrastructure.

The Imperative for Private AI

The timing for Scalytics is critical. With global data regulations becoming more stringent and AI scaling at an unprecedented rate, organizations require platforms that honor both. Scalytics empowers teams to build intelligent agents where the data resides, eliminating the need for forced centralization.

Backed by leading researchers and trusted by innovators worldwide, Scalytics is not merely building infrastructure; it is shaping the next generation of explainable, enterprise-ready AI systems.
