The Week AI Grew Up

This week, the AI world showed its hand. OpenAI made a bold move that could redefine the chip market. Europe took steps to reclaim leadership in AI mobility. China’s Zhipu AI spoke out against the hype of imminent superintelligence. Sam Altman is on a global tour building the next layer of compute infrastructure. And Citigroup raised the stakes for how much the world will spend to keep AI running. Here is what you need to know.

Featured Stories

OpenAI’s Chip Deal with AMD Could Reshape the Market

October 6, 2025 – Business Insider OpenAI signed a multi-year agreement to deploy six gigawatts of AMD GPUs, beginning with one gigawatt in 2026. The deal also gives OpenAI an option to acquire up to ten percent of AMD’s equity based on milestone performance. This partnership challenges Nvidia’s long-standing dominance and gives OpenAI greater control over its compute supply chain. Read more

Citigroup Raises AI Spending Forecast to 2.8 Trillion Dollars by 2029

September 30, 2025 – Reuters Citigroup now projects that global AI infrastructure investment will reach 2.8 trillion dollars by 2029, up from a previous estimate of 2.3 trillion. The upward revision reflects accelerated capital spending by hyperscalers and enterprise adoption. The firm expects annual AI capital expenditures to approach 490 billion dollars by 2026. Read more

Europe Moves Toward an AI-First Mobility Strategy

October 3, 2025 – Reuters European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called for an “AI first” Europe, with autonomous transportation as the first test. She proposed a network of European cities to serve as pilot programs for AI-driven vehicles, a move aimed at keeping innovation and development within Europe rather than outsourcing it to the United States or Asia. Read more

Zhipu AI Pushes Back on Superintelligence Hype

September 30, 2025 – Reuters China’s Zhipu AI CEO Zhang Peng said that true artificial superintelligence by 2030 is “unlikely.” While acknowledging rapid progress, he emphasized that human-level general intelligence remains a distant goal. At the same time, the company introduced its latest large model, GLM-4.6, which improves reasoning, writing, and code generation. Read more

Sam Altman Seeks Global Funding for Compute Expansion

October 4, 2025 – Reuters OpenAI CEO Sam Altman is touring East Asia and the Middle East to secure funding and chip partnerships to expand compute infrastructure. Samsung and SK Hynix have already signed letters of intent to supply components for new OpenAI data centers. The initiative aims to strengthen OpenAI’s global supply chain and meet growing model demand. Read more

Tools of the Day

Camel AGI

camelagi.thesamur.ai/conversation Camel AGI allows users to observe two AI agents collaborate in real time to solve assigned tasks. It is an innovative demonstration of agent cooperation and problem solving that shows what multi-agent systems might look like in the near future.

Perplexity AI

perplexity.ai Perplexity AI combines conversational search and real-time web access with clear source citations. It is particularly useful for researchers, analysts, and writers who need up-to-date and verifiable information.

TeamAI

teamai.com TeamAI is designed for businesses and collaborative teams. It allows users to share prompts, co-create AI outputs, and access top models such as GPT-4 and Claude without managing individual API keys. A clean, team-focused workspace for professional AI use.
