First things First, Prompt your Prompt.

That's right. Before you do anything, know what you want to do. Then, use a tool to help you write it clearly. Everything about AI is centered around clarity. You must be clear with what you are asking the computer to do. Even if it is a vague concept in your mind, you at least have to do the work of outlining your end goal so the tool can help you get there.

Think commands. When a general orders a soldier to do something, they do (unless they wanna get dishonorably discharged), same with AI. The only thing is, if the soldier doesn't know what to do (AI in this instance), it will still do "something" just not exactly what the General wants. Causing chaos and anger and confusion. Don't be a bad General; give the soldier what it needs to obey you.
