Once Upon an Entrepreneur | Katrina Parris

Created on 2016-11-01 17:44

Published on 2016-11-01 17:48

Katrina is the owner of NiLu, a boutique gift shop located in Harlem, New York. Prior to NiLu, she owned and sold Katrina Parris Flowers, a top rated NYC floral business.

Occupation: Business Owner

Tell us about your background. Where were you born/raised and what has influenced you towards the path you’re on?

Born in Boston, MA - I have been an entrepreneur for over 14 years. Coming from a large family instilled at an early age that, "I am the boss of me". I have always been creative and wanted to share my personal creativity as well share with others the things that I discover, but created by others.

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you decided to pursue your path?

The sudden death of my mother was my "Aha" moment --- Life is short.

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you knew you had chosen the right path?

There are moments that you feel you have chosen the right path, they have ebbs and flows. Unfortunately, we live in a world where the monetary outcome, sometimes determines if the path you have chosen, is the right one.

What are the most difficult lessons you’ve learned?

That you must always choose to give from your heart, because you will not always be compensated and/or receive reciprocity.

How is dealing with success different than dealing with other experiences?

Success is relative. And when you are an entrepreneur, you rarely get the time to enjoy it, you are always thinking of the next move.

If you’re having a bad day, how do you keep yourself up?

If I am having a bad day, I try and take some time just for me. If I am able to get on my bike and take a ride, stop at a cafe and take a moment or go to the gym and exercise, it helps me keep grounded.

What constitutes a good day?

Gym, getting dressed casually, having a thoughtful and leisurely breakfast, taking in a new experience (exploring a neighborhood, store, restaurant), having lunch a favorite spot or discovering a new place, meeting my husband for a cocktail before dinner and then having dinner with my family (2 sons and husband).

Who would you like to have coffee with most and why?

my father - he died three years ago and there are still so many questions I would like to ask him.

On a scale of 1-10, how important is (a) a team (b) a good idea ( c) a strategy (d) organization?

10 is the answer to all the questions - They are all equally important- in the words of Marcus Lemons, "People, Product, and Process."

If Napoleon Hill was asking you the secret right now, what would you tell him?

In the words of William Shakespeare - " This Above All: To Thine OwnSelf be True
