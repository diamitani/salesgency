Video Summary

Sebastian Mertens shared his journey into automation and AI, starting from Python scrapers to founding and selling automation agencies in Germany and Eastern Europe. Frustrated by Zapier's limitations, he became one of the first users of Integromat (now Make). He eventually joined Make after the CEO reached out, recognizing his deep expertise. He explained how his businesses used automation internally—optimizing operations from onboarding to project delivery using tools like Airtable. He shared stories like automating a dairy farm with NFC tags and emphasized the power of AI agents in Make. Sebastian encouraged beginners to explore automation through trial and error and predicted continuous improvements in AI tools, urging businesses to stay curious and engaged.

Key Points

Background & Entry into AI/Automation:

  • Got into automation in 2015–2016 using Python, Puppeteer, Django.

  • Became the first Zapier partner in Germany but later preferred Make (formerly Integromat).

  • Founded a successful automation agency in Germany, later expanded to Eastern Europe.

  • Sold both companies and briefly enjoyed post-exit downtime before joining Make.

Why Make.com?

  • Make offered more flexibility than Zapier.

  • Make allows more scalable, customized automation workflows, critical for complex use cases.

Favorite Automation Projects:

  • Early projects included automating business processes end-to-end—from ads to onboarding.

  • A standout project involved automating a dairy farm with 10,000 cows using NFC ear tags, weight machines, and Airtable, enabling early detection of sick cows.

Internal Automation at His Agency:

  • Automated customer touchpoints, ad optimization, onboarding, and project assignment.

  • Minimal back-office staff—relied on automation for lean operations.

  • Created templates to rapidly deploy tailored automations for new clients.

Advice on Building Automations:

  • Start with small scenarios.

  • Use trial and error to learn; documentation and community resources help.

  • Automate tasks you do often, not rare edge cases.

  • Focus on reusability—create scenarios you can plug into larger agents.

On AI Agents & Tool Use:

  • Agents let you use human-language prompts to execute real tasks using tools (e.g., Gmail, Slack).

  • Unlike raw LLMs, agents in Make can perform actions on your behalf using connected tools.

Make for Beginners:

  • Example: If you want to send Gmail messages to Slack without copying manually, Make can do that.

  • Automates moving data and processes between platforms—no coding required.

Advice to Small Businesses/New AI Users:

  • Stay curious—AI is evolving fast.

  • Today’s best model will feel outdated in the near future.

  • Understand AI’s foundations to make it work for your business.
