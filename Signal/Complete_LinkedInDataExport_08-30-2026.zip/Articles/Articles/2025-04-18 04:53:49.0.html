🤖 OpenAI Pushes Toward AGI with o3 and o4-mini OpenAI has launched two of its most advanced models yet: o3 and o4-mini, calling the update a "GPT-4 level qualitative leap." These models not only outperform prior benchmarks but also offer:

  • Multimodal Reasoning: They "think with images" by integrating visual analysis into their chain of thought.

  • Full Tool Access: They can utilize ChatGPT’s suite (web, Python, image generation) simultaneously.

  • Codex CLI: An open-source coding agent that connects terminal-based workflows to GPT-level intelligence.

Greg Brockman claims the models are capable of "producing novel scientific ideas," signaling a step closer to AGI. Source

💻 Microsoft Copilot Now Uses Your Computer Like a Human Microsoft has added "computer use" functionality to Copilot Studio. This enables agents to directly interact with desktop applications and websites — clicking buttons, filling forms, and navigating menus just like a person would.

  • Uses GUI interaction to bypass API limitations

  • Reacts to interface changes in real-time

  • Maintains privacy: enterprise data isn’t used in training

Source
