As a Managing Principal of Epos Global Management, a real estate development firm. Mike's primary role is the negotiation and syndication of each of Epos’ successful deals. In addition, Mike currently serves on the Queens Community Board No. 7 (Greater Flushing area), the Board of Directors for the Greater Flushing Chamber of Commerce, Flushing Y, and KALCA (Korean-American League for Civic Action).​

Occupation: Real estate

Tell us about your background. Where were you born/raised and what has influenced you towards the path you’re on?

NY- [The book] Rich Dad Poor Dad 

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you decided to pursue your path?

After I read the book

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you knew you had chosen the right path?​

When I discovered how wealthy people maintain their wealth through RE

What are the most difficult lessons you’ve learned?

Overcome failure by trying again with no loss of enthusiasm 

How is dealing with success different than dealing with other experiences?

You need to learn how to grow stronger mentally and emotionally to carry bigger loads analogous to lifting weights at the gym because life gets heavier and only gets harder if you don't get stronger 

If you’re having a bad day, how do you keep yourself up?

Exercise, Visualizations, Affirmations, Meditations - repeat every morning 

What constitutes a good day?

The daily and Progressive realization of my spiritual, relationship, family, and career goals

Who would you like to have coffee with most and why?

I never drink coffee but I'd like to sit down with Obama or any of the Nobel peace price winners

On a scale of 1-10, how important is (a) a team (b) a good idea ( c) a strategy (d) organization? 

10, 6, 10

If Napoleon Hill was asking you the secret right now, what would you tell him?

Have the guts to decide on what you want, then practice visualization enough until you really believe you can achieve those goals and keep visualizing the goals coming true until they really come true

How can people contact you, if at all?

SMS or email (not provided)
