Now onto the AI News of the Day:

Microsoft Will Soon Integrate its Copilot AI Into the Xbox App — and Eventually Into Your Xbox Games

Microsoft is making significant moves to enhance the Xbox experience by integrating its AI Copilot, starting with the mobile app and eventually extending to games themselves. This integration aims to provide players with helpful features, but it also sparks discussions about its effects on the gaming experience and user privacy. | Source

Amazon Uses Arsenal of AI Weapons Against Workers

According to research by Teke Wiggin of Northwestern University, published on March 13, 2025, in The American Prospect, Amazon leverages AI-driven "algorithmic management" to monitor and discipline workers, including sending anti-union messages via handheld devices, adjusting productivity quotas strategically during the election to influence perceptions, and surveilling employees' social media to gauge union sympathies. | Source

Over half of American adults have used an AI chatbot, survey finds

A January 2025 survey by Elon University’s Imagining the Digital Future Center found that 52% of U.S. adults have used AI large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT (72% usage) and Google’s Gemini (50%), with 34% of users engaging daily. The survey of 500 adults revealed that 51% use LLMs for personal tasks, such as casual conversation (9% citing companionship as a primary use), while over 50% report productivity gains at work, using tools like Slack and PowerPoint. | Source

Archer Taps Palantir's AI to Supercharge Electric Aircraft Production and Flight Tech

Archer Aviation Inc. and Palantir Technologies Inc. announced a partnership to integrate Palantir’s AI tools, including the Palantir Foundry and AI platform, into Archer’s electric aircraft production and flight technology. This collaboration aims to enhance manufacturing at Archer’s facilities in Georgia and Silicon Valley, improve software solutions, and innovate aviation systems like air traffic control and route planning for greater efficiency, safety, and affordability. | Source

Alibaba Launches Updated Artificial Intelligence Agent As Chinese AI Competition Heats Up

Alibaba Group has launched an updated version of its Quark AI assistant, fully powered by its custom AI models, intensifying competition in China's generative AI chatbot market. Announced on March 13, 2025, the assistant can perform tasks like academic research, document drafting, image generation, and travel planning, boasting over 200 million users.
