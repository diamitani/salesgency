This is a series of interviews that I conducted by using Google Forms. I asked 10 questions that I wanted to know and the entrepreneurs that responded I've highlighted.

Kyle Mark Ryan is a partner at Ryan Media Lab, a communications and strategy firm for drone technology. 

Occupation: Partner at Ryan Media Lab

Tell us about your background. Where were you born/raised and what has influenced you towards the path you’re on?

Proudly raised in Pittsburgh. I have always been very entrepreneurial since I was a kid. Whether it was selling t-shirts I made, creating media content, starting a social media consulting company with one of my closest friends in College. I have always wanted to CREATE and have the ability to CREATE for a living. My parents have influenced me the most in my life and I am beyond grateful for their love and support everyday. My parents are extremely hard-working, creative, and have always supported my sisters and I in following our passions.

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you decided to pursue your path? 

My goal has and always will be to CREATE and more specifically, that my creations and collaborations with others would impact the world in a positive way. 

When did the moment spark, if at all, where you knew you had chosen the right path?

When I began working with my Father in 2013, right after I graduated from undergrad, I knew we would do something incredible together. My family has always been extremely important to me and to be able to work alongside my parents and sisters is a dream come true. Because at the end of the day, I am working with people that I love and it constantly helps my outlook on life and work. 

What are the most difficult lessons you’ve learned?

Good things don't just happen, they take time. 

How is dealing with success different than dealing with other experiences?

Peaks and valleys are unavoidable, I just try my best to manage both when they happen. 

If you’re having a bad day, how do you keep yourself up?

Being thankful for my family and friends around me. I am very blessed to have such amazing people in my life. 

What constitutes a good day?

Knowing I tried my best and continued to make progress on my goals.

Who would you like to have coffee with most and why?

George Washington. He founded and led one of the greatest startups in history.

On a scale of 1-10, how important is (a) a team (b) a good idea ( c) a strategy (d) organization?

a. 10 b. 7 c. 8 d. 8

If Napoleon Hill was asking you the secret right now, what would you tell him?​

Be kind

How can people contact you, if at all?

kyle@ryanmedialab.com and all social media my username is @kylemarkryan
