1. Meta Unveils Standalone AI App Built on Llama 4

Meta Platforms has launched a new standalone AI application powered by its Llama 4 model, aiming to compete directly with OpenAI's ChatGPT. The app features a "discover" feed showcasing user interactions with AI and a voice mode for communication. Users can link their Facebook and Instagram accounts to enhance personalization. Meta's open-source approach allows free access to its AI tools, with over a billion users engaging monthly. The announcement was made at Meta’s inaugural LlamaCon conference, where CEO Mark Zuckerberg discussed AI advancements with Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella. Source

2. AI Now Writes Significant Portions of Code at Major Tech Firms

At the 2025 LlamaCon conference, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella revealed that AI now writes 20% to 30% of the code for certain company projects, depending on the programming language. Google CEO Sundar Pichai stated that over 30% of new code at Google is AI-generated, up from 25% in October 2024. Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg projected that within a year, about half of the company’s Llama model development could be handled by AI. This shift is leading to increased productivity and changes in engineering roles across the tech industry. Source

3. Duolingo Transitions to "AI-First" Model, Replacing Contract Workers

Language-learning app Duolingo is shifting to an "AI-first" model, aiming to replace contract content creators with artificial intelligence. CEO Luis von Ahn announced that AI will increasingly be used for content creation, hiring, and performance evaluations. This strategic pivot is part of a broader plan to boost scalability and support Duolingo’s subscription-based "freemium" model. The company anticipates 2025 revenues to reach between $962.5 million and $978.5 million, surpassing Wall Street's forecasts. Source

4. Huawei Introduces AI Data Lake Solution to Accelerate Industry Intelligence

At the 4th Huawei Innovative Data Infrastructure (IDI) Forum in Munich, Huawei unveiled its AI Data Lake Solution, designed to accelerate industry intelligence. The solution aims to provide enterprises with efficient data storage and processing capabilities, facilitating the integration of AI into various industrial applications. Source

5. Scientists Use AI to Monitor Rapid Glacier Loss in Arctic Norway

Researchers are employing artificial intelligence to track the rapid loss of glaciers in Svalbard, a cluster of Norwegian islands north of the Arctic Circle. AI models analyze satellite imagery to monitor changes in glacier size and movement, providing critical data on the impacts of climate change in the region. Source
