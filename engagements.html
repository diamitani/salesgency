Fit, Both Ways

Who we decline.

A retainer is a real commitment on both sides. We say no upfront rather than take a check we can't deliver against.

  • Teams without a named budget owner at the CEO or CRO level who can approve a six-figure annual engagement.
  • Anyone looking for a rented SaaS seat rather than infrastructure they'll own outright when the engagement ends.
  • Organizations unwilling to grant engineering access to their CRM and revenue stack — we build inside your systems, not around them.
  • Companies under roughly $2M ARR without a clear near-term path past it. The Studio, not a retainer, is the honest starting point there.
