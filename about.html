About SalesGency

SalesGency is an in-house GTM agency for companies that would rather own their revenue engine than rent it. We're engineers first: we design GTM systems, build them on modern automation and AI, and hand you 100% of the code.

What we believe

  • Architecture over tools. The stack serves the system, not the other way around.
  • You own everything. No retainer lock-in, no seat tax, no black box. The code, prompts, and runbooks are yours.
  • Deterministic data, agentic judgment. LLMs only where judgment matters; deterministic pipelines everywhere else.
  • Humans approve. Autonomy with a brake pedal — nothing customer-facing sends without your sign-off.

The ROSTR framework

Everything we build runs on ROSTR: intent → evidence → jobs-to-be-done → orchestration → documentation → architecture → GTM → taste → quality. It's the backbone of our skills, our build packages, and the GTM Agent portal.
