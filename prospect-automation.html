System Architecture

The 5-Pillar 9-Node Production Pattern.

Every PAE workflow is built upon our canonical production graph: combining deterministic data routing with agentic research synthesis.

Pillar 01

Trigger Ingest & Normalizer

Ingests real-time webhooks, scheduled daily CRON sweeps, or CSV uploads. Normalizes company domains, cleans names, and standardizes payload schemas.

Node 01 & Node 02 Latency < 0.2s
Pillar 02

CRM Shield & Dedupe Gate

Mandatory safety gate. Queries Salesforce/HubSpot before contacting any prospect. Halts automatically if an active deal, open pipeline stage, or client tag exists.

Node 03 Zero Collision
