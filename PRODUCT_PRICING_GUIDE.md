An intensive, structured co-building sprint where we design, architect, and live-prototype your revenue automation engine alongside your team.

- **Part 1 (2× 1-Hour Sessions)**:
  - *Session 1 (60m)*: Business intake, offer & deal economics, bottleneck extraction.
  - *Session 2 (60m)*: GTM architecture mapping, data waterfall selection, tool keysheet setup.
- **Part 2 (4× 30-Minute Sessions)**:
  - *Sessions 3–5 (30m each)*: Rapid prototyping rounds (scraper setup, dedupe shield, prompt fine-tuning).

#### Sprint Working Rhythm
1. **15-Minute Intake**: Extract target ICP, ACV, and CRM constraints.
2. **15-Minute Architecture**: Engineer node topology, dedupe logic, and schema.
3. **30-Minute Live Prototype**: Execute end-to-end workflow on real prospect data.
