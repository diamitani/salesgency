Execution Process

How the Custom Engagement Works.

From intake to verified production delivery in as little as 72 hours.

STEP 01 · DAY 1

Intake & Discovery

We deeply analyze three areas via call or intake form: Your Business (value prop, differentiators, deal size), Your Customers (ICP titles, trigger events), and Your Tech Stack (CRM, outreach tools, data gaps).

STEP 02 · DAYS 2–3

Construction & Assembly

Once intake data and system credentials (keysheet) are received, we begin construction of your personalized PAE graph, wiring API tokens, CRM shields, and PAS copywriting prompts.

STEP 03 · DAYS 3–5

Validation & Delivery

We validate all integrations, run end-to-end sandbox tests, collect final credentials, and deliver the complete package: n8n workflow JSON, engineer skills, and bonus failure monitoring.

⚡ 3 Ingestion Trigger Options

  • 1. Skill to Webhook: Upload a SalesGency skill to your LLM; send prospect data directly to the automation via webhook.
  • 2. Daily Trigger: Scheduled pull from your CRM or spreadsheet at a set time each morning.
  • 3. Custom Agent: Fully autonomous — scans for ICP-matched companies, builds prospect lists, and runs the entire flow.

🎯 2 Automation Types

  • Company Automation: You have target company domains but no contacts — the system finds decision makers, enriches verified emails/mobiles, and reaches out.
  • Contact Automation: You already have contacts in a spreadsheet or CRM — the system skips the people-find step and executes research and personalized PAS copy directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need n8n?

For the Custom Engagement and DIY Package, yes — n8n is the automation backbone. SalesGency can set up your self-hosted n8n instance as part of the engagement. For the Custom Agent, SalesGency can host the environment for you.

What if I don't have any contact data?

The Company Automation path is designed for exactly this scenario. You provide a list of target companies (or an ICP description), and the system finds, validates, and enriches contact information before reaching out.

Can I use my existing CRM?

Yes. All major CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Pipedrive, Zoho, Monday, ClickUp) are supported. The CRM Dedupe Shield will always check your existing records before any contact is touched.

What data tools do I need?

The minimum viable stack is one enrichment tool (Clay, Apollo, or ZoomInfo) and one outreach platform. SalesGency will advise on the best combination based on your budget and ICP.
