FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we own 100% of the deployed code?

Yes. All n8n workflow blueprints, prompt files, custom scripts, and documentation belong to your organization permanently without recurring software lock-in.

What tech stacks do you support?

We build directly inside HubSpot, Salesforce, Clay, Apollo.io, Smartlead, Instantly, n8n (self-hosted or cloud), and Claude / OpenAI / Gemini API harnesses.

How does the $999 GTM Teardown work?

You get a comprehensive audit of your current stack, mapping out data flows and bottlenecks. If you decide to proceed with a Sprint or a Foundation retainer, the $999 is fully credited toward that engagement.

Why does a retainer cost more than a rented agency?

It doesn't rent anything. A named retainer buys dedicated engineers building owned infrastructure inside your stack. When the engagement ends, the code, data, and workflows stay yours. See the full breakdown on Engagements.
