Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Client Data & Infrastructure Isolation

SalesGency builds custom revenue workflows deployed directly into client private cloud environments (n8n, AWS, GCP, Vercel). We do not aggregate, train global AI models on, or resell your proprietary prospect or customer data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect contact information submitted via our intake and booking forms (name, work email, company website, CRM details) solely to schedule intro consultations and deliver services.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Credential Vaults

When designing automations, all API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, HubSpot, Salesforce, Apollo, Smartlead) remain within your private credentials vault. SalesGency never stores or logs client secret tokens in public repositories.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For privacy inquiries, contact us at privacy@salesgency.com.
