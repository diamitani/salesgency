Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Client Data & Infrastructure Isolation

SalesGency builds custom revenue workflows deployed directly into client cloud environments (n8n, AWS, GCP, Vercel). We do not aggregate, train global AI models on, or resell your proprietary prospect or customer data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect contact information submitted via our intake and booking forms (name, work email, company website) solely to schedule intro consultations and deliver services.

3. Third-Party Integrations

When designing automations, all API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, HubSpot, Salesforce, Apollo, Smartlead) remain within your private credentials vault. SalesGency never stores or logs client secret tokens in public repositories.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact us at privacy@salesgency.com.
