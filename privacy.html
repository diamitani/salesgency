Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you download a free skill or template, nothing is collected — the files are served directly. When you contact us or book a call, we receive the information you provide (name, email, company, CRM, team size). When you purchase, our payment processor (Stripe) handles your payment details; we never see or store your card number.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry, deliver your purchase, and — only if you opt in — send occasional updates about new skills and templates. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties except the processors needed to run the site (hosting, payments).

Portal sign-in

The GTM Agent portal uses a receipt-email trust model: your purchase receipt email is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) to unlock portal access. It never leaves your device.

Cookies

We use no tracking cookies beyond what's required for checkout and basic analytics.
