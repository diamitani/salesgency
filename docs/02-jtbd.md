## 2. Secondary Jobs by Persona

### 2.1 The Growth Marketer / Junior SDR
- **Situation:** "I am launching a new outbound campaign and need high-converting copy frameworks."
- **Action:** "Download the free Cold Email Masterclass and 50 B2B Prompts or deploy the PAS Copywriter Skill."
- **Outcome:** "Launch personalized campaigns in < 15 minutes with verified sub-85 word structures that pass spam filters."
- **Switching Trigger:** Frustration with generic ChatGPT outputs that sound like marketing fluff.

### 2.2 The RevOps Specialist / GTM Engineer
- **Situation:** "My CRM is flooded with duplicate leads and manual Clay-to-Smartlead exports are breaking."
- **Action:** "Install the CRM Hygiene Plugin ($199) and Firmographic Enricher ($199) into our n8n cluster."
- **Outcome:** "Automated 24/7 lead de-duplication, waterfall enrichment, and instant trigger routing."
- **Switching Trigger:** Expensive enterprise integration tools (Zapier Enterprise, Make Enterprise) failing at volume.

### 2.3 The Growth-Stage Founder (Series A / Seed)
- **Situation:** "We just raised capital and need to prove predictable outbound pipeline before hiring an expensive VP of Sales."
- **Action:** "Book a 14-Day Build Sprint ($2,999) or 30-Day Turnkey Sprint ($4,999)."
- **Outcome:** "Live, autonomous Prospect Automation Engine operating across email, LinkedIn, and CRM within 2 weeks."
- **Switching Trigger:** Quotations from traditional agencies asking for $10k/month retainers + 6-month lock-ins.

### 2.4 The Midmarket VP of Sales / CRO ($20k/mo envelope)
- **Situation:** "We have 15 SDRs spending 60% of their day doing manual research instead of selling."
- **Action:** "Retain SalesGency as our Fractional GTM Engineer ($19,999/mo)."
- **Outcome:** "Full revenue engineering pod running continuous optimization, custom scrapers, and pipeline automation."
- **Switching Trigger:** Inability to hire senior GTM engineers ($250k+ salary each) and siloed sales tools.
