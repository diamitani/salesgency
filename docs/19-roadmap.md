## 2. GTM Distribution & Channel Strategy
1. **Product-Led Growth (PLG):** Free Cold Email Masterclass and 50 B2B Prompts drive top-of-funnel inbound signups and email captures.
2. **Modular Upsells:** $199 Skill Plugins provide immediate low-risk utility for SDR managers and RevOps engineers.
3. **High-Ticket Conversion:** 1-Hour Build Session ($999) acts as a high-trust pilot that converts into 14-Day ($2,999) and 30-Day ($4,999) Sprints, credited 100% toward the larger engagement.
4. **Midmarket Retainers:** $19,999/mo Fractional GTM Engineer tier captures companies with $20k/mo marketing/sales tech budgets looking for a complete engineering pod.
