# 17. SalesGency Security & Privacy Specification

## 1. Threat Modeling (STRIDE Analysis)

| Threat Category | Potential Vector | Mitigation in SalesGency Architecture |
|---|---|---|
| **Spoofing** | Attacker forging Stripe webhook events | HMAC-SHA256 signature verification via `stripe.webhooks.constructEvent` with `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. |
| **Tampering** | Modifying price or product parameters in client | Price IDs and amounts are strictly resolved server-side in `api/_catalog.js`. Client product slug is validated against allowlist. |
| **Repudiation** | Disputes over subscription purchases | Full audit log generated by Stripe Checkout and immutable receipt generation on `/checkout-success.html`. |
| **Information Disclosure** | Exposing API secrets in client bundles | Zero client secret exposure. `.gitignore` strictly protects `.env*`. Client receives only `pk_live_...`. |
| **Denial of Service** | Flooding session creation endpoints | Edge rate-limiting and lightweight serverless functions with fast response times (<200ms). |
| **Elevation of Privilege** | Unauthorized access to admin portal | Customer portal session tokens are single-use, short-lived URLs generated directly via authenticated customer IDs. |

---

## 2. Privacy & Data Protection Controls
- **Zero Lead Retention Policy:** SalesGency does not store client lead contact lists, CSVs, or prospect data on its own servers. All enrichment happens in client-owned n8n environments.
- **GDPR & CCPA Alignment:** Privacy policy (`privacy.html`) explicitly outlines that user session cookies are strictly functional and no third-party tracking pixels harvest customer data.
- **PCI DSS SAQ-A Compliance:** No credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank details ever touch SalesGency servers; all payments execute on Stripe-hosted infrastructure.
