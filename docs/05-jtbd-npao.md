## 1. Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

### Primary Job
> **When** I need to build out an automated sales pipeline or CRM workflow,  
> **I want to** type my system requirements in plain language into an intelligent builder,  
> **So that I can** generate and test production n8n workflows, AI skills, and agent souls instantly, without weeks of manual coding or RevOps consulting fees.

### Secondary Jobs
1. **Template Acquisition:** When I need proven sales templates (speed-to-lead, waterfall scraping, email copy), I want to preview, buy, and download them in under 2 minutes.
2. **Interactive Sandboxing:** When I want to verify an automation before putting it into production, I want to see the agent's CLI execution and payload in a safe sandbox terminal.
3. **Skill & Prompt Injection:** When I want to customize my own LLM agents, I want exportable `SKILL.md` files calibrated with sales guardrails.
