Production Capabilities

Engineering precision for B2B pipeline generation.

Say goodbye to spammy generic templates and wasted SDR hours scraping spreadsheets. This workflow turns live buyer intent into high-converting booked meetings.

🎯

Trigger-Based Intent Ingest

Listens for high-converting trigger events: newly posted job openings in your niche, recent Series A/B funding announcements, and executive leadership transitions.

🌊

4-Tier Waterfall Enrichment

Queries multiple premium data vendors in sequence. If Tier 1 fails to find a valid direct inbox, it cascades to Tier 2, 3, and 4, maximizing match rate while minimizing API cost.

🛡️

CRM Shield & Deduplication

Prevents your reps from emailing existing active clients, currently negotiating prospects, or recently unsubscribed contacts by querying your CRM database in real time.

✍️

AI PAS Copywriting Engine

Uses Claude 3.5 Sonnet with strict anti-slop rules (no "I hope this email finds you well", no fluff). Structures every email around Problem, Agitate, and Solve with specific company hooks.

📬

ZeroBounce Deliverability Guard

Performs SMTP handshake validation, MX record analysis, and catch-all detection before passing any contact to your sending domains, protecting your reputation at >98% deliverability.

🔄

Automated Sequencer Load & Tag

Pushes enriched leads directly into your Smartlead, Instantly, or lemlist campaigns, tagging leads with campaign IDs, intent signals, and personalization snippets.
