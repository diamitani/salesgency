# 5-Star Review Capture & Customer Delight Skill

Use this skill to transform positive customer feedback, milestone completion, and support tickets into verified 5-star public reviews.

---

## 🎯 Review Timing Trigger Rules

Never ask for a review randomly. Trigger strictly when one of these 3 events occurs:
1. **Milestone Unlocked**: First successful campaign launch, workflow deployment verified, or first 50 leads captured.
2. **Spontaneous Praise**: Client writes "You guys are amazing!", "Saved us so much time", or "This works great" in Slack or email.
3. **High NPS / CSAT**: Client rates 9 or 10 on a post-onboarding pulse survey.

---

## 📋 The 2-Step Soft Prompt Framework

### Step 1: Micro-Validation
When praise is detected in Slack or Email:
> "So glad to hear that, {{first_name}}! The team worked hard to get that latency down under 30s. Would you be open to sharing a 1-sentence quote on our Google Profile to help other GTM teams find us?"

### Step 2: 1-Click Frictionless Link
Provide direct deep-link with pre-formatted suggestion:
> "Here’s the direct 1-click link: {{Google_Review_Direct_URL}}
> 
> You could literally just paste: *'SalesGency deployed our inbound engine in 48 hours and shaved our lead response time from 45 mins to 28 seconds.'* 
> 
> Really appreciate you!"
