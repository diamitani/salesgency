The six core workflows

Battle-tested, production-hardened, and running in real pipelines.

📥

1 · Inbound Triage

Sub-30-second speed-to-lead. Intent triage, 7-tier lead categorization, calendar routing, and a Slack VIP ping when it matters.

2 · Outbound Prospecting (PAE)

The flagship engine: intent signals → CRM dedupe shield → contact waterfall reveal → AI PAS copy → sequencer. The full 9-node graph.

3 · Pre-Call Dossiers

A calendar monitor fires 10 minutes before every meeting: a 1-page dossier — prospect history, recent filings, talking points.

4 · Post-Call Processing

Call recordings ingested → LLM notes in MEDDPICC structure → CRM stage patched → draft follow-up written.

5 · Daily Execution Report

An 8:00 AM cron: stuck deals (5+ days), missing close dates, per-rep volume — a Slack scorecard every morning.

6 · Daily GTM Report

Pipeline velocity, new qualified pipeline, CAC velocity, and month-end forecast — daily and automatic.
