Intent Routing Intelligence

The 7-Class Inbound Intent Matrix.

Eliminate SDR fatigue by filtering out job applicants, existing customer support issues, and vendor pitches before they reach sales calendars.

Priority 01

🎯 Speak with Sales

Prospect actively seeking quote, demo, pricing, service expansion, or enterprise capabilities. Instantly drafts contextual email & notifies rep in Slack.

Priority 02

🤝 Partnership Enquiry

Reseller, agency alliance, co-marketing, technology integration, or channel partner inquiry. Automatically routes to Head of Alliances.

Priority 03

💼 Career Enquiry

Job applicants, CV submissions, interview verification, or internship questions. Routes to HR/Recruiting with standard acknowledgment.

Priority 04

🛟 Customer Support

Existing customer asking for portal access, payroll support, billing questions, or ticket creation. Auto-creates Zendesk / HubSpot ticket.

Priority 05

📰 Press & Media

Journalists, podcast invitations, PR spokespersons, or industry analyst requests. Routes to Communications team.

Priority 06

🛡️ General / Vendor Triage

Filters out vendor cold pitches, appointment setting agencies, lead gen spam, and unsolicited software proposals to protect SDR bandwidth.
