The Methodical Engine

The PAL & ROSTR Orchestration Loop

How POP converts simple requests into enterprise-grade system blueprints.

1. The PAL Ingestion Pipeline

  • Parse: Extracts raw entities, constraints, timelines, and budgets.
  • Ambiguity Scan: Flags missing facts and resolves contradictions.
  • Latent Intent: Infers unstated architectural requirements and edge cases.
  • Expand: Generates complete task dependency trees.
  • Compile: Compiles standardized markdown and JSON deliverables.

2. The ROSTR Quality Framework

  • Intent: Sovereign objective and business outcome.
  • Evidence: Measurable metrics and verification logs.
  • JTBD & NPAO: Jobs-to-be-Done & Necessity, Priority, Anxiety, Opportunity (rostr-paper.vercel.app).
  • Architecture: Security boundaries, API schemas, and data models.
  • Taste & Quality: Anti-slop aesthetics and 10-point audit scores.
