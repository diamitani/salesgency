Production Capabilities

Fast follow-ups close deals. Zero admin overhead.

Sales reps hate updating CRMs. Deals stall when follow-up emails take 24 hours to send. This workflow eliminates every bottleneck.

🎙️

Universal Notetaker Webhook

Ingests structured speaker-diarized transcripts from Fathom, Fireflies.ai, Gong, Otter, or native Zoom recordings the second the call finishes.

📋

MEDDPICC Qualification Parser

Parses the exact conversation for Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Metrics, Identified Pain, and Champion mentions, populating CRM fields automatically.

📬

Inbox Draft Generation

Drafts a personalized, concise follow-up email addressing the specific topics agreed on, embedding relevant case studies and scheduling next steps directly in the rep's email drafts.

🔄

Bi-Directional CRM Deal Sync

Updates deal stage progression (e.g., Discovery → Demo Completed), modifies close date based on stated timeline, and updates deal size estimates.

🎯

Action-Item Ticketing

Extracts technical commitments made on the call (e.g., "send custom security docs" or "verify SAML SSO") and creates tickets in Linear or Asana assigned to the SE team.

💬

Slack Leadership Notification

Posts an executive 3-bullet summary to the leadership Slack channel with sentiment score, deal risk flags, and next milestone.
