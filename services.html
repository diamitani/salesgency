⚡ 4 Core GTM Pillars

Engineered to Help Organizations
Close More Sales

Sales Gency is a GTM AI & Automation Agency.
We build custom workflows, skills and agents across Inbound, Outbound, RevOps, and Enablement so your team scales without adding headcount.

💬
Pillar 01

Inbound Automation

We build ai and automation tools to manage your inbound lead process. From custom chatbots to MQL classification workflows, we increase speed to reply time, ensure more qualified leads are sent to two inboxes and track all incoming messages.

Custom Chatbots & Forms Smart conversational AI that asks qualification questions, extracts buyer requirements, and schedules meetings in real time.
MQL Classification Workflows Automatic enrichment and intent scoring matching your ICP before the lead hits the sales queue.
Sub-Minute Speed to Reply Instant Slack/SMS notifications, automated personalized confirmation notes, and lightning-fast calendar links.
Dual Inbox Routing & Tracking Simultaneous delivery to assigned rep and leadership oversight inboxes with complete event telemetry.
Triad Deliverable for Inbound:
Workflow: Webhook Intake & Dual Dispatch Skill: MQL Scoring & ICP Filter Agent: Conversational Qualification Bot
🎯
Pillar 02

Outbound Automation

We develop custom outreach automation engines designed to reach your target customer, draft a relevant message and analyze the right signals to research properly.

Target Customer Signals Automated scrapers monitoring job postings, funding events, leadership hires, tech stack shifts, and regulatory deadlines.
Dynamic PAS AI Copywriting 3-sentence Problem-Agitate-Solve copy referencing specific prospect data without sounding generic or AI-generated.
Waterfall Reveal Engine Cascading verification across Apollo, Clay, Hunter, and custom databases for 98%+ phone and email deliverability.
Deliverability Shield Multi-domain rotation, SPF/DKIM/DMARC hardening, ramp schedules, and sender reputation monitoring.
Triad Deliverable for Outbound:
Workflow: Signal Trigger & Sequencer Enrollment Skill: PAS Framework & Signal Prompting Agent: Prospect Research & Reply Triage Agent
📊
Pillar 03

RevOps

We help with data hygiene and CRM optimization to help your revenue engine grow. Includes import formatters, deal and product updates, quote and ticket submissions and more.

Import Formatters Automated scripts normalizing inconsistent CSVs, trade show scans, and web scraping lists into clean CRM fields.
Deal & Product Updates Bi-directional syncing between CRM stages, billing engines, and product inventory catalogs.
Quote & Ticket Submissions One-click automated quote generation, PDF compilation, contract delivery, and support ticket dispatch.
Data Hygiene & Deduplication Automated sweeps detecting duplicate companies, merged contact cards, and standardizing MEDDPICC fields.
Triad Deliverable for RevOps:
Workflow: Bi-Directional CRM Webhook Sync Skill: CSV Sanitizer & Field Transformer Agent: Contract & Ticket Dispatcher
📚
Pillar 04

Enablement

We make sure all of your tools are set up, your training is built and the playbook is executed. Tech stack and key sheet organization, strategy and tactics, resources and courses.

Tech Stack & Key Sheet Organized credential management, API key maps, webhook endpoint dictionaries, and architectural diagrams.
Strategy & Tactics Battle-tested ICP definitions, objection handling scripts, cold call frameworks, and reply conversion trees.
Resources & Courses Video walkthroughs, step-by-step rep SOPs, interactive prompts, and living knowledge bases.
Playbook Execution Live shadowing, rep calibration, weekly metric review, and continuous prompt refinement.
Triad Deliverable for Enablement:
Workflow: Onboarding Portal & Knowledge Base Sync Skill: Playbook Prompt Vault & Scripts Agent: Rep Copilot & Call Summary Bot
