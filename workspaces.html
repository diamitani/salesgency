Client workspaces

Your GTM engine, running in its own workspace.

A workspace is the private operating environment where your SalesGency engine lives, runs, and is governed — not a dashboard, the actual system. Blueprints, run history, approval gates, and reporting, all in one place, wired to your CRM and tools. For teams who bought a template, booked a build session, or run an agency sprint.
