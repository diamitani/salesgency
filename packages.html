The productized skill loop

Every build runs on one loop: you talk, the harness builds, the outputs come back to you.

1 · User input

You describe what you need in plain conversation — no forms, no spec docs. The intake lives in chat, where the context already is.

2 · Webhook to skill / harness

Your words route straight into the backend Builder agent's skill harness. It synthesizes your workflows, skills, and agent souls against your stack — compiling, testing, and documenting as it goes.

3 · Webhook back to chat

The outputs land back in your conversation: importable .n8n.json files, SKILL.md packs, soul definitions, playbooks. Download, import, activate — yours to keep.
