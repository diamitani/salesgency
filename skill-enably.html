The 7-step enablement framework

Needs assessment

Interviews reps and managers to find what's actually missing — not what HQ assumes.

Content audit

Inventory by stage, persona, and format. Find the gaps and the graveyard.

Content roadmap

Battle cards, one-pagers, discovery questions, ROI models — mapped to the buyer journey.

Training design

30/60/90 ramp, workshops, and certification paths.

Coaching framework

Manager cadence, call and deal reviews — enablement for managers, not just reps.

Tooling

Seismic, Highspot, Showpad — or a lightweight taxonomy if you're not there yet. Anti-"content graveyard" discoverability.

Measurement

Content usage, time-to-first-deal, win rate by content engagement, rep ramp time.

Outputs — what you get

  • Enablement strategy — the full plan, buyer-journey aligned.
  • Playbooks & battle cards — content reps actually use.
  • Talk tracks & competitive intelligence — what to say, and what to say about competitors.
  • Onboarding curriculum — 30/60/90 with certification.
  • Coaching cadences — manager rituals that compound.
  • Enablement ROI metrics — proof the program works.
