Production Capabilities

Every sales rep briefed like an industry veteran.

Eliminate frantic last-minute Googling. The Pre Sales Call Automation aggregates all pertinent executive context into an actionable one-page briefing.

👤

Executive Career Trajectory

Maps the buyer's previous leadership roles, past employers (which helps identify common tech tool exposure), and exact time in role to anticipate decision authority.

🏢

Hiring Telemetry & Headcount

Scrapes currently open job requisitions. If an account is hiring 5 SDRs or 3 RevOps analysts, the rep knows exactly where budget is currently being allocated.

💻

Detected Software Stack

Identifies the prospect's frontend, CRM, marketing automation, analytics, and sequencing tools via DNS and HTTP header scans, avoiding awkward compatibility questions.

❓

MEDDPICC Discovery Angles

Uses Claude 3.5 Sonnet to formulate 3 to 5 pointed discovery questions tailored to the company's size, business model, and competitive threats.

⏰

T-10 Minute Slack Notification

Delivered via Slack DM or a dedicated #deal-intel channel exactly 10 minutes prior to call start time, giving the AE the perfect window to review key facts.

📁

Automatic CRM Account Attachment

Writes the full dossier into the CRM deal notes (HubSpot/Salesforce), ensuring that sales managers and solutions engineers have immediate visibility into research.
